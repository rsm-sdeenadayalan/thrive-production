# Where to find MSBA plans of study, course schedules and syllabi

Rady keeps the current plans of study and program schedules for every graduate
program in a shared Google Drive folder called **Rady Graduate Program
Schedules and Resources**:

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NkOVx60spVY31IHWZ7KWlGXBiXLL6Xk6

Inside it, the **Current Plans of Study** folder is organised by program start
year (2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 starts). For each year it holds the plan of
study for each program, including the MSBA 11-month, the MSBA 17-month and the
MSBA Flex (part-time) schedules, alongside the other Rady graduate programs
(EMBA, Full-Time MBA, FlexEvening/FlexWeekend MBA, MPAc, MQF and MFin).

# Signing in to the Rady program Drive folder (SSO)

The folder is not public. Opening the link while signed out, or while signed in
with a personal Google account, returns a permission error rather than the file
list. To get in:

1. Sign in to Google with your **UC San Diego account** (your `@ucsd.edu`
   address), which authenticates through UCSD single sign-on (SSO).
2. If your browser is signed in to several Google accounts at once, use the
   account switcher and pick the UCSD one — a personal account will be refused
   even though the link is correct.
3. Open the Drive link again.
4. If you still see "Request access", use that button, or ask MSBA advising to
   share the folder with you.

# Finding the syllabus or course schedule for a specific MSBA course

Start with the Rady program Drive folder, which holds the plans of study and
program schedules for every Rady graduate program:

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NkOVx60spVY31IHWZ7KWlGXBiXLL6Xk6

That folder is not public — sign in to Google with your UC San Diego
`@ucsd.edu` account, which authenticates through UCSD single sign-on (SSO). If
you are signed in to several Google accounts, switch to the UCSD one, because a
personal account is refused even though the link is correct. If you land on
"Request access", use that button or ask MSBA advising to share the folder.

A plan of study in that folder lists the course codes, titles, unit values and
the quarter each course is taken in — that is what tells you *which* courses
make up your program and *when* you take them. It is not the same as a course
syllabus.

For the syllabus of one specific course — the week-by-week topics, assignments,
grading breakdown and instructor policies:

- Check the course site for that course, which is where the instructor posts
  the syllabus and course materials.
- Ask the course instructor directly if the syllabus is not posted yet, which
  is common before a quarter starts.
- Ask MSBA advising if you need a syllabus for a course you are not enrolled in
  yet — for example when deciding between electives, or for a transfer or
  credit-evaluation request.

# How many units does the MSBA degree require?

Every MSBA plan of study — 11-month, 17-month and Flex part-time alike —
requires the same totals to graduate:

- Total units required to graduate: 50 units
- Core courses: 22 units
- Elective courses: 28 units

This requirement is the same across all MSBA cohorts, so it is stated here once
rather than repeated in each plan of study.

# What the plan of study can tell you without signing in

THRIVE already holds the current MSBA plans of study, so you can ask directly
rather than opening Drive — for example which courses are in a given quarter,
how many units a quarter carries, which quarter a specific course is taken in,
or how the 11-month and 17-month schedules differ.

THRIVE holds the plans for cohorts that are still enrolled: the 11-month plan
for the MSBA 2027 cohort (Summer 2026 to Spring 2027), the 17-month plan
starting Summer 2026, and the 17-month plan starting Summer 2025. Plans for
cohorts that have already graduated, and the plans for the other Rady graduate
programs, are in the Drive folder above.

Each published plan of study is a *sample* plan. Students are responsible for
adhering to and understanding all policy and degree requirements, so confirm
your own schedule with MSBA advising before you enrol.
