# Zoom: Set Up Your UCSD Pro Account

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27. These dates already passed as of 2026-08-24 — historical record, not upcoming.]

UC San Diego provides enrolled students with access to a Zoom Pro account. You can use Zoom to host virtual meetings, collaborate with classmates, and schedule group project discussions.

> **Important:** Do not create a separate Zoom account at Zoom.us using your UCSD email address. To access your UC San Diego Zoom Pro account, sign in through UC San Diego using Single Sign-On (SSO).

---

## Step 1: Claim Your UC San Diego Zoom Pro Account

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27. These dates already passed as of 2026-08-24 — historical record, not upcoming.]

1. Visit [ucsd.zoom.us](https://ucsd.zoom.us/).
2. Select **Sign In**.
3. Use your UCSD Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials.

Once you sign in through UC San Diego, your account will be connected to UC San Diego's Zoom environment and upgraded to the available Zoom Pro license.

**Need more information?** Review [UC San Diego's Zoom information page](https://blink.ucsd.edu/go/zoom) for additional setup guidance and support resources.

---

## Step 2: Add Zoom to Google Calendar

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27. These dates already passed as of 2026-08-24 — historical record, not upcoming.]

This step is optional, but it makes it easier to schedule Zoom meetings for group projects and other activities directly from your UCSD Google Calendar.

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Install the Zoom Scheduler browser extension. You can review [UC San Diego's Zoom Scheduler installation instructions](https://support.ucsd.edu/its).
3. Open your UCSD Google Calendar.
4. Select **Create**, then choose **Event**.
5. Add the meeting title, date, time, and the email addresses of the people you want to invite.
6. Select the **Make it a Zoom Meeting** button.

Look for this button in your Google Calendar event:

> More options · Save · **Make it a Zoom Meeting**

## Key Takeaway

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27. These dates already passed as of 2026-08-24 — historical record, not upcoming.]

Always access Zoom through UC San Diego using SSO. Installing the Zoom Scheduler browser extension is optional, but it gives you a quick way to create Zoom links directly from your UCSD Google Calendar.
