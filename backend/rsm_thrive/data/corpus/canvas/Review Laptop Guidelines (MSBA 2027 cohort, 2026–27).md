## 💻 2026 Laptop & Technology Guidelines

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27 guidance, as captured 2026-08-17.]

Having the right laptop will make your experience much smoother—whether you are attending class, analyzing data, collaborating with classmates, or completing projects.

We know you are here to earn a master's degree—not become an IT expert. This guide outlines the laptop specifications, software, and technology setup you will need for success at Rady.

📅 **When to purchase:** We recommend buying your laptop within 30 days of your program start date. This may help you access back-to-school discounts while ensuring that you receive current-generation technology.

## ⚠️ Before You Purchase

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27 guidance, as captured 2026-08-17.]

- Avoid ultra-budget or ultra-thin laptops that use low-power processors such as Intel Core i3 or Core M processors. These devices may not adequately support analytics, programming, and intensive multitasking.
- Do not purchase a new laptop with only 8 GB of RAM. It is not sufficient for Rady graduate coursework.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27 guidance, as captured 2026-08-17.]

**Do I need to buy a new laptop?**
Not necessarily. If you purchased your laptop within the last year and it meets the minimum requirements, it will likely be sufficient. We strongly recommend confirming that it has an extended warranty.

**Can I use a work-issued laptop?**
We do not recommend it. Employer-issued devices often restrict administrative access, software installation, VPN use, email configuration, or other features required for coursework.

**Do I need to purchase Microsoft Office?**
No. Microsoft 365—including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint—is [provided to UC San Diego students at no additional cost](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c0VrIGAKHyg).

**What language should my computer and software use?**
Your operating system and Microsoft Office should use U.S. English. Rady Technology Services supports only English-language systems and software.

**Where can I purchase a laptop?**
You may purchase your laptop from any retailer. The UC San Diego Bookstore and many major manufacturers and retailers offer student discounts.

- UC San Diego Bookstore (discounts for [Dell Laptops](https://www.ucsandiegobookstore.com/technology/computers/dell-laptops) and [MacBook Pro](https://www.ucsandiegobookstore.com/technology/apple/macbook-pro))
- Apple
- Dell
- Lenovo
- Best Buy
- Costco

**Does Rady recommend a particular brand?**
No. You may choose any manufacturer as long as the laptop meets or exceeds the minimum hardware and software requirements.

**Why do MSBA students need a higher-performance laptop?**
The MSBA program includes data-heavy and computationally intensive work, including machine learning, programming, model inference, data pipelines, and large datasets.

A lower-powered computer may work well for Canvas, Excel, and presentations but may become a significant bottleneck during MSBA coursework.
