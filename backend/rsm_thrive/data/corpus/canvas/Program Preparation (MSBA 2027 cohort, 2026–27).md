## 1. Complete Your DataCamp Assignments

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27. These dates already passed as of 2026-08-24 — historical record, not upcoming.]

A successful career in analytics requires a strong programming skillset. DataCamp will help ensure that everyone enters the program with foundational experience in the tools and programming languages used throughout the MSBA curriculum.

Required topics include:

- Python
- SQL
- Bash shell
- ChatGPT

Complete **all assigned activities** in the MSBA DataCamp group.

> **Open the MSBA DataCamp Group** — [https://rady.instructure.com/courses/2062/assignments/25766](https://rady.instructure.com/courses/2062/assignments/25766)

Use your UC San Diego email address when creating or accessing your DataCamp account.
