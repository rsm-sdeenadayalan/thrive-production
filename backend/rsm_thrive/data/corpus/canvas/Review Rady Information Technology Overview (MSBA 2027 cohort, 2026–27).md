## 💻 Rady Information Technology Overview

[MSBA 2027 cohort, 2026–27. These dates already passed as of 2026-08-24 — historical record, not upcoming.]

Everything you need to know to get your technology set up before classes begin.

Your laptop and technology setup are essential to your success at Rady. In this presentation, the Rady Information Technology team explains the systems, accounts, software, and support resources you will use throughout your program.

Please watch the complete recording and review the presentation slides before Orientation. Completing these steps in advance will help ensure that you are ready to access your accounts, connect to campus systems, and begin classes.
