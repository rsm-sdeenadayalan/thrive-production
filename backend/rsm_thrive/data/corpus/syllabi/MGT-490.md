# MGT 490 — Mergers, Acquisitions, and Corporate Restructurings

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered FA26

## What this course covers

This course explores major corporate reorganizations including acquisitions,
divestitures, and restructurings, and how firms use them to create shareholder value in
response to competitive challenges and opportunities. It provides a framework for
analyzing transactions through in-depth case studies, covering valuation, accounting,
taxation, governance, cross-border deals, and buyouts.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- mastering the language and process of m&a and restructuring
- identifying problems and opportunities in corporate reorganizations
- analyzing transactions rigorously and understanding drivers of success or failure
- forming educated opinions about proposed deals
- applying accounting and taxation concepts to acquisitions and restructurings
- understanding the regulatory framework of corporate acquisitions
- presenting a case analysis of an m&a transaction

## Topics covered

history and economic role of corporate acquisitions, synergy and growth, valuation, accounting and taxation of acquisitions, consolidation and productive efficiencies, managerial incentives and corporate governance, cross-border acquisitions, leveraged buyouts, management buyouts.

## Tools used

microsoft excel, microsoft powerpoint, canvas.

## Prerequisites

Knowledge of the concepts of finance developed in MGT 408, basic familiarity with Microsoft Excel.

## How it is graded

Grading is based on quizzes (72 points, best six of seven), a team research presentation (18 points), and class participation (10 points).

DESCRIPTION
This course explores major corporate reorganizations:
acquisitions, divestitures, and
restructurings. Firms make these strategic decisions in face of major competitive challenges
and opportunities to increase shareholder value by exploiting growth and repositioning
opportunities. Studying corporate reorganizations gives powerful insights to how firms adapt to
technological change, economic and regulatory shocks, and globalization as well as the roles
economic institutions play in promoting competition and mandating disclosures.
The course provides a framework for analyzing transactions based on in-depth analysis of case
studies.
Prerequisites: Knowledge of the concepts of finance developed in MGT 408, basic familiarity
with Microsoft Excel.

ASSIGNMENTS & DELIVERABLES
Reading Assignments
This course is based on in-depth analysis of business case studies. Students will be expected
to read carefully the assigned case study prior to class.
Team Projects
Students will form teams of 3 to 5 members to analyze a case study in M&A. Students can
either choose a business study case from a list, or develop a “live” case study by analyzing a
recent transaction. Teams are encouraged to supplement information in the case studies with
their own research of the companies and relevant issues. Teams will prepare a 20-25 minute
Powerpoint presentation for the last session.

Collaboration and use of resources and applications besides those specified by the
instructor are not allowed on the quizzes.
The Honor Code of Rady School applies to all quizzes.
Quizzes are worth 12 gradepoints each. The lowest quiz grade will be dropped.
If a student is expecting technical difficulties in accessing Canvas or email during the time of the
quiz, (s)he should write an email to the instructor before the time of quiz and request a
makeup quiz. Makeup requests are not accepted once the quiz is made accessible to students.
Class Participation
Class participation is an important way to exchange ideas and opinions, and raise the quality of

Ten gradepoints are awarded for class participation. This grade will reflect my assessment of
both the quantity and quality of the individual’s contribution to class learning.
Excused absences are permitted up to 20% of the class sessions. Students granted an
excused absence are expected to catch up with the material they miss by watching class
recordings and working through the material independently. They may request a makeup quiz.
