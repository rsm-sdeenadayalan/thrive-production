# MGTA 463 — Fraud Analytics

**4 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered SP26

## What this course covers

Teaches building statistical and machine learning models to detect fraud: raw data
analysis and cleaning, expert feature engineering, algorithm selection, and model
evaluation. Real-world supervised and unsupervised problems include credit card
transaction fraud, NY property tax anomalies, and application identity fraud.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- end-to-end fraud detection pipelines in Python
- cleaning and profiling large dirty datasets
- feature engineering for fraud
- anomaly detection on unlabeled data
- writing professional business reports

## Topics covered

modes of fraud, data quality reports, feature engineering, supervised vs. unsupervised fraud models, ML algorithms (trees, random forests, boosting, neural nets), model evaluation, fuzzy matching and linking.

## Tools used

Python.

## Prerequisites

Good background in math and statistics and working ability in Python.

## How it is graded

10 homeworks (130 pts) plus participation (10 pts); curved (roughly half As).

## Workload

Heavy. Ten individual homework assignments with weekly deadlines, including three substantial report-based projects on real datasets.
