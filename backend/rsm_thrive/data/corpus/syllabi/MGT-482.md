# MGT 482 — Brand Management

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered SU26

## What this course covers

A case-based MBA course examining brands as co-creations of consumers and marketers, and
the strategic tasks required of brand managers. Topics include brand equity,
architecture, extensions, storytelling, licensing, crisis management, and luxury
branding. Emphasis is on applying frameworks to real-world cases through discussion and
participation.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- analyzing brand strategy
- evaluating brand equity
- managing brand extensions
- crafting brand stories
- navigating brand crises
- leveraging brand licensing
- applying brand management frameworks to cases
- integrating social media into branding strategy

## Topics covered

brand equity, brand architecture, brand components, brand extensions, lifestyle brands, brand life cycle, brand storytelling, brand licensing, brand crisis management, luxury branding.

DESCRIPTION
A brand is often a company’s most important asset and as such has received immense attention in recent years.
Effective brand-building and brand management drives superior financial results, consumer loyalty and competitive
advantage. This is a course with a decided point-of-view on the nature of brands and the skills and tasks this requires
of managers. We will consider brands as co-creations of consumers and marketers. If brand management is a
collaborative process of meaning management, the job of the brand manager then becomes one of navigating the
meaning making processes, attaining meanings that resonate therein, and to managing these meanings deftly
through time so as to maximize brand value, capture opportunities, and diminish risk. Some of a firm’s most valuable
assets are the brands that it has invested in and developed over time. Although manufacturing processes can often
be duplicated, strongly held beliefs and attitudes established in consumers’ minds cannot.
This is a case-based course. Cases, as microcosms of brand management, deliver the power of real-world, applied
settings and reveal concepts and frameworks that can inform management thoughts and actions. Our collective
experience and expertise outweighs and overshadows the knowledge any one faculty member can impart. Case
discussions, and the full participation they require, is an integral component of this course.

ASSIGNMENTS & DELIVERABLES
Quizzes
Each week will begin with a quiz to test whether you understood the readings for the week. These are also important
because we will not cover everything in the reading in class unless there are either issues with the concept or if the
class finds that area particularly worth exploring. We will often use the quizzes as the jumping-off point for the
evening’s discussion. Because of this, quizzes will usually be at the beginning of class. Missed quizzes may be made
up the following week before class. If you need to make up a quiz for a missed class, please show up 15 minutes
before the scheduled class time.
Exercises/Participation
Class participation is earned by “attending” class by contributing your thoughts and questions to the various
discussions and exercises in class. In practice, great ideas that are not articulated (or are not articulated in a
compelling way) are ignored. Thus, participation is important to your continued professional development. Further,
much of the value of an MBA comes from the interchange with classmates who come from various industries and
strategic contexts. The benefits that each of you receive from this class will be directly enhanced by the mutual
sharing of insights. Thus, throughout the term, I will track the degree to which you contribute to discussion. Please
know that quality of contribution (meaningful insights that help us progress in our discussion of the topic) far
outweighs quantity. Missed exercises/participation cannot be made up except in extreme circumstances.
Final Exam
The final exam will consist of two parts. The first part will be individual. It will primarily measure the knowledge
acquisition throughout the quarter. The second part will be groups. It will primarily measure knowledge application
and consist of group analysis of small cases.

AI POLICY FOR MARKETING MANAGEMENT COURSE (written with the help of ChatGPT)
This policy outlines the appropriate use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the Marketing Management course,
ensuring that the technology enhances learning while preserving the integrity of individual thought and effort. The
policy applies to all enrolled students in the Executive MBA Marketing Management course.
Permitted Use of AI:
o Understanding and Research: Students are permitted to use AI tools to deepen their understanding of
course materials and assist with research related to course topics. AI can be utilized to summarize
readings, provide explanations of complex concepts, and generate ideas for project brainstorming.
o Explicitly Allowed Assignments: AI may be used in completing assignments only when explicitly stated in
the assignment instructions. These will be designed to specifically leverage AI capabilities in a way that
enhances the learning objectives of the task.
Prohibited Use of AI:
o General Assignments and Examinations: The use of generative AI is strictly prohibited in the completion
of regular assignments and during examinations. Assignments are crafted to assess individual
understanding and the ability to apply marketing concepts independently.
o Discussion Contributions: Participation in course discussions, whether online or in-person, must be free
of AI assistance. The value of these discussions lies in personal insights, original thought, and interaction
among peers, which AI usage could undermine.
Rationale:
o The educational value of assignments and discussions in this course stems from personal intellectual
engagement and the development of individual analytical skills. AI, while beneficial in certain contexts,
can shortcut these learning processes and reduce the depth of personal understanding and critical
thinking.
Enforcement:
o Students are expected to adhere to this policy as part of their commitment to academic integrity.
Violations of the AI usage policy will be subject to review under the standard academic misconduct
procedures and could result in disciplinary action.
Support and Clarifications:
o Students are encouraged to seek clarification from the instructor regarding the use of AI tools in specific
instances. The instructor is available to provide guidance on how AI can be appropriately integrated into
learning activities without compromising educational outcomes.
