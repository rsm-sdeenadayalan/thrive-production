# MGT 470 — Digital Disruption

Rady MBA · offered WI26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

Advanced graduate course on digital business model innovation using customer-centric
principles. Students learn to identify disruption opportunities, assess risks, and build
customer-centric business models using decoupling and the Customer Value Chain
frameworks. Taught through Harvard case discussions, interactive presentations, and
hands-on group activities.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- identifying opportunities for disruption
- assessing risks of disruptive business models
- designing customer-centric business models
- applying decoupling and customer value chain frameworks
- building growth strategies for digital businesses
- calculating customer acquisition costs and lifetime value
- analyzing case companies' digital strategies
- presenting a disruptive business model

## Topics covered

digital business models, disruption opportunities, customer value chain, decoupling, online platforms, direct-to-consumer startups, monetization strategies, customer acquisition costs, retention and growth, freemium pricing.

## Tools used

canvas, chatgpt, large language models.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Class participation 40%, assignments 20%, and final project 40%.

## Workload

Heavy. Weekly Harvard case readings and chapter readings, assignments roughly every three weeks, and a final project to design a disruptive business model.

Policy on AI, gen AI and LLMs
I will assume all students will have access to and use generative AI and LLMs (chatGPT and its clones
chatPDF, LLama, Bard, etc.) as their own personal tutor. Anything incremental is their own work and
will contribute to their grades. In practice I intend to use Chat GPT to feed my Harvard cases and ask it
for summaries and to answer assignment questions about the case/company. I will tell that to students.
And I may start class discussions with whatever the genAI tool spits out. That will be the baseline
understanding. Any student that delivers in class or in homework at exactly that level will get a
maximum grade of a C- or D. In other words, they just learned basic genAI prompts and to memorize
the output. For work handed in, I ask students the courtesy to disclose gen AI as a source like any
other source on the internet or otherwise.

IN SUM, I explicitly allow any use of large language models like ChatGPT or others. Be advised
that their accuracy is often poor, so please be careful, as you remain solely responsible for the
accuracy, content and conciseness of your submitted answers. I view LLMs as good sources of ideas
and pointers, but poor sources of accurate information and VERY poor substitutes for human
judgment.
