# MGT 428 — Managerial Judgment and Decision Making

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered SP26, SP27

## What this course covers

This course examines how people make judgments and decisions, focusing on uncertainty
and variability, and how to design effective decision processes. It then shifts to
building, protecting, and exerting influence to implement decisions and achieve goals.
The course is taught through exercises, case discussions, and lectures.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- designing effective decision processes
- evaluating judgment and decision-making biases
- applying behavioral insights to business strategy
- building and exerting influence
- expanding personal influence tactics
- persuading stakeholders
- navigating organizational networks

## Topics covered

judgment and decision making, uncertainty and variability, heuristics and biases, group decision making, decision making via models and algorithms, risk taking, influence tactics, coalitions, networks, persuasion.

DESCRIPTION
This course is composed of two parts. They are quite distinct yet critically connected: the first is
about deriving better analyses and solutions, and the second is about implementing them.
In our initial sessions, we will examine how people make judgments and decisions, concentrating on
how people perceive and react to uncertainty and variability. We will lay out key implications for
business strategies. Consider a biotech start-up that is attempting to develop a new cancer drug.
Suppose its managers must choose between divergent research tracks and market niches, each of
which shows some promise but also carries some risks. How will they weigh the promise versus the
risks, evaluate relevant information, and settle on a way to proceed? The hope is that a savvy
understanding of the answers to such questions can inform your approach to many business
settings.
Of course, it is not enough to be skillful at analysis. To succeed in business, you must also be adept
at engaging others and getting their buy-in and follow through. For instance, the managers of the
biotech start-up may wish to motivate employees and other stakeholders to devote their energies to
a particular research approach. They may wish to rally investors around funding this approach.
Accordingly, in our latter sessions, we study how to best build, protect, and exert influence to
achieve your goals. A key idea is that developing multiple tools of influence is necessary because
different circumstances call for different tools.

OBJECTIVES

•

At the close of this course, you should better understand
 How people think about uncertainty and variability
 How to design effective decision processes
 What influence tactics you tend to rely on
 How to construct a plan for expanding the set of influence tactics you use

GRADING
Your grade will be based on participation (25%), a midterm (35%) and a final project (40%).
PARTICIPATION (25%)
Classroom discussion is an integral part of this course. Much of your learning will come from
the insights we are able to achieve by collectively mulling course concepts. Moreover, to fully
engage with the course material you need to be actively involved in the discussion.
Because it is vital to your learning and the learning of your colleagues, your participation is
assessed and factored into your grade. I make notes after each class regarding each student’s
role in the discussion. So does the teaching assistant. Near the end of the term, I will solicit
your input about which students best-facilitated your learning.
We are all jointly responsible for the quality of the discussion. I would like for you to focus on
being prepared, engaged and respectful of others. We want to cultivate a direct but friendly
environment that encourages honesty and risk-taking. Mistakes are okay, especially as long
as we are striving for insight. Irreverence is okay. Questions others know the answer to are
okay. As long as you are prepared, engaged and respectful, almost anything goes.
Everyone should participate. This means some of you will have to fight your natural tendencies
– those accustomed to speaking might need to back off, those accustomed to not speaking will
need to step up. This gives everyone a chance to learn from others’ experiences, as well as a
chance to work on effective communication.
Finally, your on-time and proper completion of the surveys and other assignments for each
class will facilitate our discussions and thus contribute substantially to what you and your
colleagues learn. It thus also factors into your grade.

other than DS. You sense that if you don’t persuade her this time, your fate is sealed.
