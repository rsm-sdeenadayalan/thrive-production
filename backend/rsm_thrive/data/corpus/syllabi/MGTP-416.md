# MGTP 416 — Ethics in Accounting and Business

Rady MPAc (Accountancy) · offered WI26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

This course provides a strong basis for identifying, evaluating and responding to
ethical issues encountered as an accounting and business professional. It covers ethical
theory, ethical reasoning, and professional codes of conduct including the AICPA, SEC,
TSBPA, and IMA. Students also explore integrity, objectivity, independence, corporate
governance, and ethical management.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- applying ethical reasoning to dilemmas
- complying with professional codes of conduct
- evaluating a company's ethical performance
- identifying ethical issues in business news
- making decisions about ethical dilemmas
- developing ethics proposals
- reflecting on personal ethics

## Topics covered

ethical theory, ethical reasoning and decision-making, professional codes of conduct, integrity in accounting, objectivity and independence, corporate governance, ethical management, privacy ethics, ethical leadership.

## Tools used

canvas, powerpoint.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Final grade is based on 80 points of assignments and 20 points of exams, with individual work weighted 67.5% and team assignments 32.5%.

## Workload

Heavy. Graded work includes individual and team assignments, two exams, speaker reflections, an ethics simulation, and a capstone ethics pitch contest.

DESCRIPTION
Being an accountant carries enormous responsibility. You have a duty to your clients, your colleagues
and the public to act with integrity and honesty. This course will provide you with a strong basis for
identifying, evaluating and responding to ethical issues you will encounter as an accounting and business
professional. This includes review of various professional codes of conduct (AICPA, SEC, TSBPA and
IMA).

APPENDIX A: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
General
Academic Integrity is expected of everyone at UCSD. This means that you must be honest, fair,
responsible, respectful and trustworthy in all your actions. I will not tolerate lying, cheating or any other
forms of dishonesty because such actions undermine learning and the University’s ability to certify your
knowledge and abilities. Thus, I will report to the Academic Integrity Office any attempt to get, or help
another get, a grade by cheating, lying or dishonesty, which will result in sanctions, up to and including an
F in this class and suspension or dismissal from the University. So, please think carefully before you act
and ask yourself the following questions:
1. Is my action honest, fair, respectful, responsible and trustworthy?
2. Is my action authorized by the instructor?
If you are unsure, don’t ask a friend – ask me, an instructional assistant or the Academic Integrity Office.
(Source: Tricia Bertram Gallant, Ph.D., UCSD Academic Integrity Office, 2017)
You can view the complete UCSD Policy on Integrity of Scholarship at:
http://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/policy.html
You are expected to read the UCSD Policy on Integrity of Scholarship and act in accordance with the
Policy.
For additional information about UCSD’s Academic Integrity program, please go to this link:

Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) for Coursework
The use of AI tools such as ChatGPT, Bing Chat, Bard, Claude, etc. is encouraged in this course. These
tools have considerable potential to streamline and augment our work and you should use them for this
class with the following caveats and conditions:

These tools should augment your work, not replace it. For instance, large language models can

If you use Generative AI for an assignment, be transparent about if/how it was used in a footnote
or citation (e.g., link to the ChatGPT chat you created).
Large language models have documented issues with hallucination and inaccuracy (e.g.,
generating fake citations, failing logical arguments, misrepresenting sources, etc.). You are fully
responsible for the content you submit in your assignments and will be graded accordingly.
