# MGTF 418 — Topics in Finance: Preparing for the CFA Exam

**2 units** · Rady MS Finance (MFin) · offered FA26

## What this course covers

This course prepares students for the Level 1 CFA exam by covering each of the ten major
topic areas tested, including ethics, quantitative methods, financial statement
analysis, corporate finance, equity investments, fixed income, derivatives, alternative
investments, portfolio management, and economics. Students engage in applied valuation
work similar to actual CFA exam questions, use the Bloomberg Terminal, and learn study
habits of successful past candidates.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- applying industry-standard investment valuation tools
- preparing effectively for the cfa level 1 exam
- using the bloomberg terminal
- using a cfa-approved financial calculator
- applying the cfa institute code and standards

## Topics covered

ethics, quantitative methods, financial statement analysis, corporate finance, equity investments, fixed income, derivatives, alternative investments, portfolio management, economics.

## Tools used

bloomberg terminal, ti baii, hp12c, canvas.

## Prerequisites

This class assumes you are a registered CFA level 1 candidate for the 2026 or 2027 exam, and as such will have access to the CFA-required readings.

## How it is graded

Grades are based on class participation, canvas quizzes, and weekly in-person in-class quizzes, totaling 100 points.

## Workload

DESCRIPTION
Up for a challenge? Only 39% of candidates attempting L1 exam passed the May 2026 exam.
The Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation is the industry-accepted gold standard for
financial analysts. To earn the CFA charter you must pass three very challenging exams.
Exam material draws from surveys of practicing analysts and covers 10 major topics. We will
look at each of the 10 major topics areas (one per class) and explore the level of understanding
that is required to pass the first level exam. We will do applied valuation work as tested on the
actual CFA exams as well as highlight study habits of successful past candidates. We will pay
special attention to sub-topics that aren’t part of the core RADY MFIN program but that are
covered on the CFA exams.
