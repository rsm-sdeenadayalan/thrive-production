# MGTP 443 — Global Taxation and Business Strategy

**4 units** · Rady MPAc (Accountancy) · offered WI26

## What this course covers

Examines the foundations of corporate international tax strategies for US multinational
corporations, including the tax economics of corporate formations, operations,
reorganizations, liquidations, and entity classification. Covers US and international
jurisdictional tax topics that support effective business decisions and help reduce risk
while increasing after-tax cash flows. Taught through lectures, readings, discussion
problems, quizzes, and a final exam.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- identifying international tax issues in corporate structuring
- analyzing tax consequences of entity classification
- applying us international tax rules to multinational operations
- evaluating transfer pricing and cost sharing arrangements
- comparing pre- and post-2017 international tax planning
- calculating tax accounting for property and corporate transactions
- assessing global tax initiatives' impact on business strategy
- advising on tax-efficient business decisions

## Topics covered

tax accounting, property transactions, passthrough entity taxation, corporate tax operations, corporate formations and reorganizations, international taxation of multinational corporations, foreign tax credits, transfer pricing and cost sharing, hybrid transactions and tax rate arbitrage, global tax initiatives.

DESCRIPTION
MGTP 443 Global Taxation is a four-credit course that counts towards your MPAc degree requirements. Global Taxation
offers an examination of the foundations of corporate international tax strategies that are of interest to the “C” suite
and Board Members, with a focus on US multinational corporations. We will focus on the tax economics of corporate
formations, operations, reorganizations, liquidations, and related international tax consequences of corporate
structuring alternatives and entity classification consequences.
Your grade for this course is determined by (i) 25% - your attendance and participation in the sessions, (ii) 25% - effort
level on discussion problems, (iii) 25% - quizzes, and (iv) and 25% - the final exam.
Documenting your participation is an important part of your academic record. Your registration and sign-in at the
sessions you attend this quarter will become part of your official academic record.
OBJECTIVES
This class will provide you with a foundation of US and international jurisdictional tax topics that help support and drive
effective business decisions. As we strive for high level understandings of the topics that will allow you to issue spot, we
will have fun along the way, learning how multinational corporations navigate an ever-changing international tax
landscape to reduce risk while returning increased after-tax cash flows.
