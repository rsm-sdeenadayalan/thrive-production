This course examines fundamental accounting concepts and principles, focusing on
understanding and analyzing financial statements. It uses interactive lectures,
discussions, and short cases involving corporate financial statements to illustrate
accounting concepts and explore disclosure strategies.

DESCRIPTION
In this course, we will examine fundamental accounting concepts and principles. The course is intended
to provide you with the basic skills necessary to understand and analyze financial statements as well as
to prepare you for more advanced financial statement analysis courses. The objective is not to train you
as an accountant but rather to help you become an informed user of financial statements. Classes will
include interactive lectures and discussions about financial accounting principles and practices. Short
cases involving corporate financial statements will also be used to illustrate accounting concepts and to
explore the disclosure strategies of companies.
COURSE OBJECTIVES
At the close of this course you will be able to:
●​ Read and understand financial statements
●​ Find and analyze information provided in real world financial statements (as opposed to only text
book examples)
●​ Ask management the right questions about their financial statements
●​ Understand basic international differences in financial statements
