# MGT 410 — Competitive Strategy

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered FA26

## What this course covers

This course focuses on strategic decision making by companies in competitive markets.
The approach combines applied economic theory with empirical examples to develop
competitive advantage and game theory frameworks. Classes consist of both lecture and
discussion of applications, including case studies.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- identifying sources of profit
- analyzing industry competition
- developing competitive advantage
- evaluating strategic interactions
- formulating growth strategies
- communicating strategic recommendations
- applying game theory frameworks
- conducting five forces analysis

## Topics covered

competitive strategy, game theory, competitive advantage, industry analysis, five forces, positioning, strategic decision making, growth and innovation, market competition.

Group Case Write-ups
You may choose your study group of 4-6 people. Each group will submit 3 write-ups during the
quarter.
● A brief response to write-up questions, which we’ll post on Canvas.
● You may use bullet points. Aim for 1-2 pages (double spaced, 12-point font and 1-inchbmargins). Do not
include appendices. The motivation for short write-ups is to encourage you to think carefully about the
most relevant information to include, rather than attempting to include all information.
● Try to minimally discuss the cases with members outside your group before we talk about them in class.
This is so that we have a diverse range of perspectives, examples and arguments for our in-class
discussion, which will make for a much more interesting session.
● Upload write-ups to Canvas by the start of class.
Think of the case write-up as a tool to help you prepare for the in-class discussion of the case. In class,
you should be prepared to summarize the case, answer the case questions, and provide supporting
evidence for your argument. The write-up should be easy to read, tie in relevant theoretical material, and
draw appropriate conclusions using the frameworks we discuss in class.

Goals
The goal of these assignments is to have you practice the skills necessary to do competitive analysis on
your own. Given that, the structure of the assignment is designed to match the structure of a competitive
analysis you will do on the job.
Executives making strategic decisions often have limited time. Thus we will require these deliverables to
be very short but very substantive. Each portion of the assignment will require significant thinking and
refining. The teams that do the best will write something several times the length of the final assignment
and then whittle it down to its essence for the final submission.
Written Component
The three components of this project are: (A) a written Five Forces Industry Analysis, (B) a written
Positioning Analysis, and (C) Presentation of Problem and Recommendation of a Solution and final
presentation and slides. You will get more information about the parameters of the final project separately.
The first two written portions of the write-up should not exceed 3 pages (double spaced, 12-point font and
1 inch margins). We will only read the first 3 pages of text, so please stay within the page limit. The limit
does not include a cover page or appendices, which you can use to provide charts, figures, or other
background material, but these sections should be no longer than five (5) additional pages. The appendix
is not a catch-all for anything that might be relevant, but is to be used to carefully support your points.
Always include page numbers.
Presentation Component
The Presentation of Problem and Solution will take place on the last day of class and your team will
present a key problem your client is facing and offer a solution to that problem. This presentation should
include an executive summary slide and will also include the findings from your analyses where relevant.
Each team member is encouraged to take a role in the final presentation. Additional guidelines (e.g., time)
will be announced. Part of your participation grade in the course will involve asking questions to the other
groups when they present.

AI POLICY
I am a big fan of AI as a learning tool. It can really help things “click”. USE IT! Have conversations with it,
push back against it if what it’s saying isn’t making sense. You may use it as you prepare your write-ups,
to help you study for the exam, and as you work on your final projects. Just remember that
1)​ You won’t be able to use it on the exam. The exam is where you’ll show me you know the
material inside and out. I don’t mind if AI helped it “click” for you along the way.
2)​ You won’t be able to use it while you present and field questions during your final
presentation. Once again, I don’t mind if AI helped you prepare, but when you’re in front of the
class presenting your part of your group’s project, you want it to be clear as day that you have a
firm grasp of every point you’re saying and question you’re answering.
