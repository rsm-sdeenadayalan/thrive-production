COURSE DESCRIPTION
This accelerated 2-unit course meets California State Board of Accountancy requirements for focused
instruction in accounting and tax research. Students will learn to locate, interpret, and apply authoritative

Checkpoint Edge and CCH IntelliConnect. The course emphasizes the IRAC (Issue, Rule, Application,
Conclusion) framework, professional memo writing, and case-based exercises. Students will also explore
the emerging role of artificial intelligence and data analytics in research, preparing them for the evolving
demands of the accounting profession.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCY (AI) POLICY
This course will actively explore the role of artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including large language
models (LLMs), in accounting research. Students are encouraged to experiment with AI methods as
supplemental aids to enhance efficiency and explore emerging trends. However, authoritative sources—
such as the FASB Codification, PCAOB and AICPA standards, Internal Revenue Code, and SEC
releases—must always serve as the foundation for research assignments, memos, and case analyses.
Permitted Use: Students may use AI tools to the extent discussed in course readings and
lectures, with a focus on understanding how AI can complement but not replace authoritative
research.
Disclosure Requirement: Any use of AI in assignments must be clearly disclosed in a short note
(e.g., “AI was used to generate an outline for this memo; authoritative citations and analysis are
my own”).
Limitations: AI tools are known to produce errors or fabrications. Students are responsible for
verifying all information against authoritative sources.
Academic Integrity: Failure to disclose AI use, or submitting AI-generated work without proper
verification and citation of authoritative sources, may be considered a violation of academic
integrity standards.
This policy is consistent with UC San Diego’s guidance: AI may be used for support, but students are
expected to complete their own work, demonstrate independent analysis, and ensure the accuracy of all
submissions.
