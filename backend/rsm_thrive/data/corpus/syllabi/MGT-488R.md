# MGT 488R — Applied Market Research

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered FA26

## What this course covers

This course covers planning, implementing, and analyzing market research, with an
emphasis on hands-on application. Students move through the full research arc, from
formulating a research question and conducting exploratory qualitative work to designing
surveys and using adaptive conjoint analysis to quantify trade-offs. The course also
focuses on synthesizing multi-method results and presenting findings effectively.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- developing a market research program
- designing and conducting interviews
- designing and executing surveys
- analyzing market research results
- applying conjoint analysis
- synthesizing multi-method research
- presenting market research findings

## Topics covered

market research planning, qualitative research, interviews, survey design, survey analysis, cluster analysis, conjoint analysis, counterfactual simulation, presenting market research.

## Tools used

canvas, qualtrics, google suite, zoom, radiant, sawtooth, slack.

## Prerequisites

MBA student or consent of instructor and department stamp.

## How it is graded

Grading is based on individual assignments (10%), team weekly assignments and peer reviews (40%), and a final presentation (50%), with team grades adjusted by peer evaluations of contribution.

## Workload

Heavy. The syllabus states each week is designed to fill approximately 12 hours, with graded work including individual assignments, team weekly assignments with peer reviews, and a final presentation.

### MGT 488R Applied Market Research (Amir) FA26

Applied Market Research (MGT 488R)
Fall 2025 | Professor On Amir
Syllabus last updated July 29, 2025

The business world revolves around managerial decisions, and those, in turn, ought to be data
driven. In this course we will learn about planning, implementing, and analyzing market
research, synthesizing multi-methods research, and presenting market research results. We will
do this by actually doing it. We will go through the arc of formulating a market research
question, exploratory qualitative research, descriptive quantitative market research. We will
learn how to plan and conduct various types of interviews, learn how to design, execute, and
analyze surveys, as well as learn how to employ cutting edge tools such as adaptive conjoint
analysis to quantify attribute levels and trade-offs.
Prerequisites: MBA student or consent of instructor and department stamp.

for the challenges and opportunities of market research.
The course will incorporate learning activities designed to facilitate exploration of theoretical
concepts, followed by practical techniques.
