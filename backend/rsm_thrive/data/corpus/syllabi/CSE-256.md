# CSE 256 — Statistical Natural Language Processing

**4 units** · CSE · offered varies, varies, varies

## What this course covers

Modern statistical approaches to natural language processing: part-of-speech tagging,
word sense disambiguation, parsing, Markov and hidden Markov models, and probabilistic
context-free grammars.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- building NLP pipelines from statistical foundations
- language model fundamentals

## Topics covered

POS tagging, parsing, hidden Markov models, probabilistic grammars, statistical NLP.
