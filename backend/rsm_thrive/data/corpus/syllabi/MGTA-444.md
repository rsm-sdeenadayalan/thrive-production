# MGTA 444 — Business Analytics Consulting and Solution Design

**2 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered WI26

## What this course covers

A case-based course on designing effective business analytics solutions, emphasizing
that framing the problem correctly is the most critical part of any analytics project.
Students learn to translate business needs into solvable analytical problems, avoid
common data-science failure modes, and plan modeling, prescriptive analytics, and
deployment.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- framing and scoping business problems
- evaluating project proposals
- designing modeling strategies
- estimating ROI of analytics
- planning deployment

## Topics covered

problem framing, translating business needs into analytical problems, modeling strategy and evaluation, prescriptive analytics and ROI, solution deployment, why data science projects fail.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Four assignments (75 pts) plus attendance (25 pts); no final exam.

## Workload

Light. Four assignments (mostly 1-page reviews plus a group capstone presentation review), readings, no exams.

- **WI26** — Peterson

DESCRIPTION
Welcome to our graduate course on Business Analytics Solution Design! In this class, we'll
dive into the art and science of creating effective business analytics solutions, recognizing
that designing the right solution is both the most critical and the most challenging part of
any project. Albert Einstein's famously said, “If I had an hour to solve a problem, I'd spend
55 minutes thinking about the problem and 5 minutes thinking about solutions.” We'll also
stress the importance of thoroughly understanding and deﬁning problems before jumping
into solutions. You'll learn how to frame problems concisely, work efficiently through the
data analysis project ﬂow, and apply proven strategies to avoid common mistakes. Through
case studies and hands-on assignments, you'll gain skills needed to craft data-driven
solutions that make a difference.
