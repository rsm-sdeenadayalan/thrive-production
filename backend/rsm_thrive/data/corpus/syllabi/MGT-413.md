# MGT 413 — Operations Management

Rady MBA · offered WI26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

Operations Management (OM) focuses on the systematic design, execution, and continuous
improvement of business processes, projects, and partnerships. In today’s highly
competitive landscape, effective operations management is critical for organizations of
all types. This course moves beyond the traditional emphasis on cost minimization to
explore the fundamental challenges that organizations must address to achieve scalable,
sustainable growth and profitability.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- creating process flow diagrams
- applying little's law
- analyzing flow time
- conducting capacity analysis
- optimizing inventory management
- aligning operations with strategy
- presenting operations assessments
- analyzing business processes
- applying operational metrics
- performing flow-time analysis

## Topics covered

operations management, process analysis, little's law, flow time analysis, capacity analysis, inventory management, lean operations, supply chain management, operations strategy, flow-time analysis, inventory pooling, newsvendor model.

## Tools used

canvas, littlefield technologies, beer game, chatgpt.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

The final grade is based on class participation (10%), homework assignments (10%), Littlefield Technologies (15%), National Cranberry Case Write-up (15%), quizzes (20%), and a final exam (30%), graded on a curve.

## Workload

Heavy. Graded work includes three homework assignments, two team projects, three quizzes, a final exam, and class participation.

DESCRIPTION
Businesses of any type or size can benefit from careful analysis of the processes that comprise their
operations. This course will focus on how to understand and present a business as a series of processes
and how to analyze these processes to improve performance. Students will be introduced to the
foundations of Operations Management, with emphasis on identifying how businesses structure
operations to align with their strategic goals. Through consideration of a range of businesses, we will
identify how each makes decisions to manage operations and gain the mathematical skills needed to
analyze and improve these decisions.

• Understand the role of inventory and its impact on operations.
• Apply Lean Operations concepts to improve efficiency.
