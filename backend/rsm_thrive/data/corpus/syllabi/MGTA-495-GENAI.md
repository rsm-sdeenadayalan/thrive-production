# MGTA 495 — Special Topics: GenAI for Business

**4 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered SP26

## What this course covers

A practical course on leveraging Generative AI to create business value: when and how to
integrate GenAI into business processes and decision-making. Centerpiece is a quarter-
long group project building an 'AI-coworker' prototype that automates a real business
process, supported by modules on prompt engineering, RAG, tool use/MCP, and agent
systems.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- identifying where GenAI adds value
- building working GenAI prototypes
- prompt engineering
- communicating AI capabilities to stakeholders

## Topics covered

GenAI for data generation and analysis, prompt engineering, retrieval augmented generation, tool use and MCP, single and multi-agent systems, business process automation.

## Tools used

Claude Code, N8N, VS Code, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot.

## Prerequisites

No programming experience required.

## How it is graded

50% group project, 20% individual assignments, 30% participation.

## Workload

Moderate. Quarter-long group project with milestones, individual assignments, and weekly TODOs.

2 Course Objectives
Generative AI (GenAI) is developing at an incredible speed. Companies are eager to adopt
these new technologies but many struggle with implementation. In this course you will learn
how to leverage GenAI in real business settings.
• Business Impact: GenAI is already transforming core business functions - from enhancing
and automating business processes to supporting and enhancing data analysis. You’ll
learn which tools work best for different business problems and how to implement them
effectively.
• Practical Decision-Making: You’ll learn how to combine GenAI with existing business
processes to improve decision-making. This includes knowing when to use AI and,
importantly, when not to use it.
• Workflow Improvement: GenAI can streamline operations across departments. You’ll
learn to identify where these tools add real value and how to implement them in ways
that actually work.
• Career Development: Understanding GenAI isn’t optional anymore - it’s becoming a core
business skill. This course will give you hands-on experience with tools that companies
are actively using and hiring for.

3

• Broad Applications: The skills you’ll learn apply across business functions - from marketing and finance to operations and strategy. You’ll work on real business processes to
understand how these tools can solve problems. This is a practical course. You’ll spend
most of your time working with GenAI tools on real business problems. By the end,
you’ll be able to:
• Identify where GenAI can add value to business processes
• Design and implement practical GenAI solutions
• Make better-informed decisions about AI adoption
• Effectively communicate AI capabilities and limitations to stakeholders

11 Project
The group project will span the full quarter and is divided into multiple milestones to develop
an AI-coworker. The AI-coworker should (partially) automate and enhance a business process.
This process can be in any business area or can even combine multiple business functions (e.g.,
marketing, analytics, finance, operations, supply chain, consulting, etc. etc.).

11.1 Key Components
1. Business Process Analysis: Identify and document a real-world business process related
to your career interests, analyzing inefficiencies and opportunities for improvement.
2. GenAI Integration Strategy: Develop a comprehensive plan for integrating generative AI to enhance the selected business process, clearly delineating human and AI
responsibilities.
3. Functional Prototype Development: Create a working prototype that demonstrates
core functionality and key GenAI capabilities.
4. User Experience Design: Design a user-friendly interface with explicit mechanisms
for human oversight and feedback.

6

5. Application Delivery: Implement a prototype that demonstrates the complete workflow
with integrated GenAI.
Note: No programming experience is required. With state-of-the-art GenAI tools
plus instructor and TA support you can build (almost) anything you can envision.

12 Modules
Modules are (mostly) designed to support the group project, which is the main focus of the
class. Topics will include:
• Using GenAI for data generation, collection, cleaning, and analysis
• AI-human interaction
• Prompt engineering and best practices for working with GenAI
• Retrieval augmented generation (RAG)
• Tool-use and MCP to connect GenAI to external data sources and tools
• Single and multi-agent systems
• AI-gateways (e.g., OpenClaw)
These topics will be covered in a way that supports the group project milestones, with lectures,
exercises, readings, videos, and assignments designed to help you make progress on your project
while learning the necessary skills to create business value with GenAI.
