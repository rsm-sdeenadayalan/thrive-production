# MGTA 456 — Supply Chain Analytics

**4 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered WI26

## What this course covers

Links supply chain theory with data analytics applications: mapping a company's supply
chain, managing demand and supply-side uncertainty, and communicating improvement
strategies. Spans classical methods (newsvendor, forecasting, inventory) through last-
mile delivery, sustainability, and revenue management, with hands-on Python and Excel
work.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- mapping supply chains
- demand forecasting
- inventory modeling under uncertainty
- communicating analytical recommendations

## Topics covered

newsvendor models, demand forecasting, exponential smoothing, network design, inventory control, last-mile delivery, sustainability, revenue management.

## Tools used

Python, Excel.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

10% participation, 10% case write-up, 25% homework, 25% quizzes, 30% final project; no exams.

Moderate. Weekly quizzes, 5-7 homework assignments, group case write-up, group final project; no exams.

DESCRIPTION
This course is designed to link theory with application to use data analytics to manage modern supply
chains. The course will establish which components constitute a supply chain and how to understand the
strategies to improve their operations. We will explore both classical along with cutting edge approaches
to supply chain management. As much of supply chain management deals with uncertainty both in terms
of demand for products and supply-side uncertainty from disruptions and/or delays, we will focus on how
to use data to better account for uncertainty. We will spend time on current areas of focus within the
supply chain literature, especially on the challenges of last-mile delivery, and how certain companies and
researchers are working to overcome these challenges.

ASSESSMENTS
In lieu of exams, this course will assess student progress through four primary means: Case-write up,
Quizzes, Homework Assignments, and a Final Project.
Case Writeup
There will be a single case writeup which will be completed in groups. More details on expectations for
this assignment will be provided in class.
Homework Assignments
There will be between 5 and 7 homework assignments scheduled throughout the quarter. Students are
allowed to collaborate on these homework assignments, but must turn in their own reports and code
when requested.
Quizzes
This class will include regular quizzes, which will occur at the start of class to ensure students have
reviewed the material prior to class. These quizzes will also allow the professor to understand which
topics require additional attention. The lowest quiz grade for each student will be dropped. This will allow
students to miss one class without penalty. The professor will post an overview before each quiz,
addressing which material is fair game for that week’s quiz.
Final Project
There will be a final project to be completed in groups, and due before the start of the last class.
Guidelines for the final project will be posted on Canvas.
Final Exam
There will not be a final exam for this class.
