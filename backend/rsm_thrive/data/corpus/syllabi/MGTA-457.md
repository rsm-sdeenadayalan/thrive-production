# MGTA 457 — Business Intelligence Systems

**2 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered FA25, FA26, SP26

## What this course covers

A hands-on, project-based course on turning high-volume data from disparate systems into
actionable insights through interactive dashboards. The Fall section centers on Tableau
and Tableau Prep; the Spring section adds the Snowflake Data Cloud for scalable storage,
SQL/ELT, and data modeling. Culminates in a team dashboard project and presentation.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- designing and building interactive dashboards
- combining and cleansing data feeds
- data modeling
- presenting data stories
- SQL in Snowflake (SP section)

## Topics covered

business intelligence architecture, dashboard design, data visualization principles, ETL and data cleansing, data storytelling, Snowflake data warehousing (SP section), SQL and ELT (SP section).

## Tools used

Tableau, Tableau Prep, Snowflake, SQL.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Mostly project-based (presentation + deliverables ~85%), quizzes ~5-10%.

## Workload

Moderate. One multi-part team project (design doc, prep workflow, dashboards, presentation, final doc) plus quizzes over 5 weeks; no exams.

## Offerings

- **FA25** — Schibler
- **FA26** — Jambulapati, Vikram
- **SP26** — Jambulapati

ASSIGNMENTS
Reading Assignments
All readings will be supplied in Canvas
Team Projects
Small student teams will work together to design and implement a story of informative dashboards that
combine multiple data sources, and teams will use their dashboards to tell a story in their final
presentation. The group project will comprise four components, and there will be one deliverable for each
team:
1. Dashboard Design Document: This is a 2-5 page document with simple sketches of expected
elements and the intended interaction between those elements. The purpose of this document is
to thoughtfully consider the business use of the dashboard, the end-user’s expected capability
and needs, and then apply the principles of good design, prior to developing any views or
dashboards.
2. Tableau’s Prep Builder Workflow: will integrate two or more datasets, with at least four
appropriate cleaning components.
3. Dashboard and Presentation: At least 3 dashboards with 6 or more views (each student on the
team should individually create at least two views) will be used to tell a story about the data
analyzed in a classroom presentation of 10-15 minutes, and answer questions from reviewers.
(extra credit is available for good use of storyboard)
4. Final BI Project Document: Less than 5 pages of text, along with any appropriate images, to
describe the project goals, team roles, data sources and prep, dashboard interactions and
findings. Additionally, enhancements from the Design Document should be considered relative to
business goals, intended audience or useability.
Peer and Team Presentation Reviews
Each student will complete a short survey of each other team member, relative to quantity of work, value
of work, and degree of team co-operation.
Each student will attend four other team presentations, and provide simple feedback on those teams per
a rubric to be provided.
