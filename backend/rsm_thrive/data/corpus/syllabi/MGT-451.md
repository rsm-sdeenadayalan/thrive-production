# MGT 451 — Technology and Innovation Strategy

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered varies, varies, varies

## What this course covers

MBA elective on strategy for technology-driven businesses: how firms create and capture
value from innovation, platform and ecosystem dynamics, and competitive strategy in
technology markets.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- strategic analysis of technology markets
- framing product and platform strategy

## Topics covered

innovation strategy, platform competition, technology adoption, value capture, ecosystem strategy.
