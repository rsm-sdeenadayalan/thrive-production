# MGTP 444 — MPAc Capstone

Rady MPAc (Accountancy) · offered SP26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

MGTP 444 is a capstone course in which small teams of MPAc students work on real-world
projects with business leaders from companies, government agencies, or professional
services organizations. Students apply classroom concepts to actual financial
accounting, tax, or consulting issues and deliver findings through written reports and
oral presentations. The course emphasizes teamwork, leadership, and persuasive
communication of complex business and technical ideas.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- communicating complex business and technical ideas effectively
- crafting narrative-driven presentations
- delivering oral findings in professional forums
- working effectively in teams with diverse perspectives
- analyzing complex business issues and drawing conclusions
- integrating knowledge across functional business areas
- advising decision-makers

## Topics covered

capstone project, financial accounting, tax, consulting, teamwork, leadership, business communication, presentation skills, research.

## Tools used

canvas, google drive, zoom, linkedin, powerpoint.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Final grade is based on total performance and engagement, with weighted components including team milestone assignments, the final capstone presentation (40%), individual reflections, and classroom participation/peer evaluations.

## Workload

Heavy. The syllabus states numerous complex readings and studying are required, and grading includes ten team milestone assignments, a full-length capstone presentation via Zoom webinar, final uploads, individual reflections, and classroom participation/peer evaluations.

COURSE DESCRIPTION
Accountants build trust and confidence in capital markets all over the world. Accountants are
asked to support decision-makers as they navigate the complex demands of a growing number
of stakeholders.
Capstone projects are real-world problems and challenges. A faculty member will supervise you
and your small team of MPAc peers. You and your colleagues will work side-by-side with
business leaders of companies, including small start-ups, government agencies, corporations, or
professional services organizations.
Your Capstone Project will be a direct, hands-on experience applying concepts, methodologies,
and tools learned in the classroom to actual financial accounting, tax, or consulting issues of the
day.
Your Capstone Project will have the charter to work with industry or professional firm sponsors
to analyze and address a complex issue facing them or the profession. You and your team will
work on combining the knowledge gained through your studies with real-world applications,
integrating your experience across functional areas of a business, and delivering findings to your
business sponsors or mentors upon project completion.

COURSE OUTCOMES
The Capstone is a hands-on project designed for you to research and communicate complex
business, scientific and technical ideas effectively.
You will learn through the Capstone how to effectively communicate complex business,
scientific and technical ideas. This will be done by creating a well-written report on a complex
business issue or project, presenting your findings and conclusions, and delivering your findings
orally in a professional forum or event.
Through this effort, you will:
•
•
•

Expand your leadership presence and authenticity as a persuasive communicator of
complex issues facing business and community leaders.
Knowledgeably craft and deliver a narrative-driven presentation to inform and likely
influence decision-makers.
Develop communication skills, including gathering the facts, sorting through the rules
and regulations to be applied, analyzing, making conclusions, and delivering such advice.

COURSE PARTICIPATION
Success in a Capstone demands that you move considerably beyond your “comfort zone!” All
organizations today, especially those that manage through matrix structures, require strong
teamwork, especially working with others with different knowledge-based skills and
experiences.
Here is a summary of personal and business-related behaviors that will be observed by your
faculty sponsor and will be considered in your final grade:
•
•
•
•
•
•
•

How willing you are to explore the edges of your comfort zone.
How ready are you to handle failure in team-based decisions that you are
uncomfortable with?
Will you be open?
How effectively do you work with and/or integrate perspectives different from your
own?
How well do you help create an environment where everybody is learning and support
others’ learning, including on your team project?
How intellectually rigorous will your topic chosen be?
How well you respectfully challenge others’ expressed views (including your mentor or
instructor) and advocate for your ideas.
