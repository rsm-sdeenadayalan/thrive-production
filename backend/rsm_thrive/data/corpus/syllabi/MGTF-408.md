# MGTF 408 — Fundamentals of Real Estate Analysis

Rady MQF (Master of Quantitative Finance) · offered SP25 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

This course applies corporate finance and investment principles to real estate markets,
focusing on commercial real estate assets and a value-based framework for real estate
decisions. It covers valuation, mortgage basics, leverage, capital structure, REITs, and
urban economic and historical topics such as segregation and redlining. The course is
taught through in-person lectures, asynchronous topic recordings, and a team
presentation and write-up.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- applying corporate finance and investment principles to real estate
- building and analyzing real estate pro forma models
- valuing commercial real estate assets
- assessing how leverage affects expected returns
- analyzing supply and demand factors in real estate markets
- evaluating risk and return in real estate investments
- presenting on historical and policy issues in housing

## Topics covered

commercial real estate, space and asset markets, real estate returns and price indices, market analysis, leasing strategy and pro forma, real estate valuation, mortgage finance and leverage, real estate capital structure, real estate investment trusts, urban economics and housing policy.
