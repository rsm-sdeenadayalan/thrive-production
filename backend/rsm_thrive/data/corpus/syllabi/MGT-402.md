# MGT 402 — Data Driven Communications

Rady MBA · offered SU25, WI26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

This highly participatory course equips analytical professionals to translate complex
technical information and business insights into clear, compelling messages for diverse
audiences. Students learn techniques for high-impact presentations, including eye
contact, vocal presence, effective gestures, and clear, visually engaging slide design,
as well as data-driven storytelling. Through lecture, demonstration, creative exercises,
improvisation, peer feedback, and individual coaching, students refine their
communication for a range of business contexts.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- delivering high-impact presentations
- translating complex data into clear messages
- using eye contact, gestures, and vocal projection
- tailoring messages to diverse audiences
- creating compelling data stories
- designing effective slides
- giving and receiving feedback
- speaking spontaneously and adapting to audiences
- delivering engaging presentations
- structuring content and ideas

## Topics covered

data storytelling, presentation skills, improvisation, slide design, audience engagement, persuasive communication, leadership communication, storytelling, public speaking, visual communication, winning the first 60 seconds, authenticity.

## Tools used

powerpoint.

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Grading is based on Presentation #1 (15%), Team Presentation #1 (15%), Presentation #2 (20%), Class Involvement (20%), and Presentation #3 (30%).

Heavy. Graded work includes two individual presentations, a team presentation, and required class involvement with exercises and coaching in every session.

DESCRIPTION
In today’s lightning-fast global business environment, the ability to communicate persuasively
and to explain data effectively both in person and virtually is essential. This highly participatory
course equips analytical professionals to translate complex technical information and business
insights into clear, compelling messages for diverse audiences.
You will learn techniques for high-impact presentations, including eye contact, vocal presence,
effective gestures, and clear, visually engaging slide design. You will also develop the
transformational skills that distinguish exceptional communicators: speaking directly from the
mind and heart to influence the hearts and minds of others. Through practice, you’ll experience
the power of authentic, data-driven storytelling and build confidence in tailoring your message to
different audiences.
Drawing on approaches from communication, theater, and leadership training, the course helps
you connect with audiences in ways that are both memorable and motivating. A combination of
lecture, demonstration, creative exercises, improvisation, peer feedback, and individual coaching
will guide you in refining your data-driven communication for a range of business contexts.
Each session includes the development, rehearsal, and delivery of key messages using proven
templates and coaching methods. You will leave the course more confident, creative, and
compelling—ready to lead through communication that earns attention and inspires action.

DESCRIPTION
In our global business environment of lightning-fast communications, strong presentations skills
are an essential capability of management. As leaders you are called on to rally teams around
programs, attract interest and capital in new products, pitch clients for products and services, and
attract talented practitioners to your teams. Especially when so many communications are
delivered virtually, there is no faster way of directly influencing large numbers of stakeholders
than through effective presentations.
What are the ingredients of great presentations? What makes audiences feel engaged, moved,
and wanting to take future action? While much communications training focuses on external
techniques such as making strong eye contact, projection of your voice, appropriate gesturing,
etc., this class also explores the dramatic distinctions between merely good and truly great
presenters. Good presenters make strong eye contact. Great presenters make the listener feel
honored, engaged, and empowered. Good presenters are clear and efficient. Great presenters
are passionate and authentic.
This course defines presentations as: speaking before one or more people with a desire to
influence a future outcome. Obviously in management you are presenting all the time.
Many of this class’ instructional techniques are drawn from the art of training actors to effectively
engage audiences in theatre. Actors’ time-honed techniques for gaining standing ovations
provide a framework for presentations that engage and excite.

relationship you want to establish, as well as how

material. You can be a standing ovation presenter
