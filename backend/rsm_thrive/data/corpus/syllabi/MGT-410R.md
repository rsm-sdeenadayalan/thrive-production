# MGT 410R — Competitive Strategy

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered FA26

## What this course covers

This course focuses on strategic decision making by companies in competitive markets.
The approach combines applied economic theory with empirical examples to develop
competitive advantage and game theory frameworks. Classes consist of lecture and
discussion of applications, including case studies.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- identifying sources of profit within and across industries
- creating and sustaining competitive advantage
- directing company growth, innovation, and responses to changing markets
- analyzing strategic interactions between competing and complementary companies
- shaping industry competition to a firm's advantage
- conducting five forces industry analysis
- conducting positioning analysis
- communicating strategic recommendations

## Topics covered

five forces analysis, competitive advantage, game theory, market entry, price competition, network effects, growth strategy, global strategy, industry analysis, strategic decision making.

## Tools used

canvas, chatgpt, study.net.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Grading is based on participation (20%), group case write-ups (20%), a take-home exam (30%), and a final group project (30%).

The write-up should be easy to read, tie in relevant theoretical material, and draw

Group Final Strategy Project
The main project in the course is an analysis of a company to be completed with your team. You
should think of the final project as a more in-depth and less directed version of the case writeup. The goal of the project is to have you practice the skills necessary to do competitive analysis
on your own. Given that, the structure of the assignment is designed to match the structure of
a competitive analysis you will do on the job.
The first step will be for you as a team to select a company and strategic problem they face. The
three components of this project are:
(1) a written Five Forces Industry Analysis
(2) a written Positioning Analysis
(3) Presentation of Problem and Recommendation of a Solution
The Presentation of Problem and Solution will take place on the last day of class and your team
will present a key problem your client is facing and offer a solution to that problem. This
presentation should include an executive summary slide and will also include the findings from
your analyses where relevant. Each team member is encouraged to take a role in the final
presentation. Additional guidelines, like presentation times, will be announced.
Part of your participation grade in the course will involve asking questions to the other groups
when they present, so please be an active participant.
