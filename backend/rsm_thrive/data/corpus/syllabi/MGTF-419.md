# MGTF 419 — Special Topics in Finance: CFA Level II

Rady MQF (Master of Quantitative Finance) · offered SP26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

This course prepares CFA Level II candidates for the 2026 exam by covering the 10 major
topic areas and the deeper understanding required for the fact-pattern format. It
includes applied valuation work as tested on the CFA exams, along with Bloomberg
terminal use for real-time context and study habits of successful candidates.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- preparing for the cfa level ii exam
- applying valuation techniques tested on the cfa exam
- using bloomberg commands for real-time financial context
- navigating the cfa level ii fact-pattern exam format
- developing effective study habits for cfa exam preparation

## Topics covered

ethics, quantitative methods, portfolio management, economics, financial reporting and analysis, corporate finance, equity investments, fixed income, derivatives, alternative investments.

## Tools used

bloomberg terminal.

## Prerequisites

This class assumes you are a registered CFA level 2 candidate for the 2026 exam, and as such will have access to the CFA-required readings. Please do not sign up for this class if you have not passed L1.

## How it is graded

Pass/fail, based on class participation (50%) and quizzes (50%).

## Workload

Heavy. Weekly pre-readings and self-directed practice questions, plus graded quizzes and required class participation.

DESCRIPTION
This class is for candidates who passed CFA Level 1 exam and are preparing for the Level 2
exam. The Level 2 exam is widely considered the most difficult of the three CFA exams even
though the pass rate is slightly higher than level 1. The test format moves to a fact pattern
format that requires a deeper understanding of the material.
We will look at each of the 10 major topics areas and explore the level of understanding that is
required to pass the 2nd level exam. We will do applied valuation work as tested on the actual
CFA exams as well as highlight study habits of successful past candidates.
