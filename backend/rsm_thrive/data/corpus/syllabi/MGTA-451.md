# MGTA 451 — Business Analytics in Marketing, Finance & Operations

**4 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered SU26

## What this course covers

A three-module intensive course introducing analytics applications in marketing,
finance, and operations, each taught by a different professor. Topics span customer
value and advertising measurement, time value of money and portfolio concepts, and
process analysis with capacity and inventory fundamentals.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- applying customer value concepts
- evaluating advertising effectiveness and causal claims
- financial decision-making with NPV and TVM
- process-flow, inventory, and capacity analysis

## Topics covered

marketing and customer value, advertising measurement and causality, time value of money, NPV and investment decisions, risk and return, process analysis and Little's Law, capacity and flow time analysis.

## Prerequisites

There are no prerequisites to this course.

## How it is graded

Three module grades, each 1/3 of the total; marketing module curved; oral exams in finance and operations modules.

## Workload

Moderate. Per-module assessments: quizzes and exercises (marketing), group homework plus take-home and oral exams (finance), homework plus written and oral exam (operations).

## Offerings

- **SU26** — Buti, Shahsavand, Wilbur

3.​ Assessments and Grading
The marketing module component will count for ⅓ of your MGTA 451 grade. The marketing

4.​ Communication
We meet in person to facilitate two-way open-ended interactions. You should bring your
questions, comments and observations to the class, to test your understanding, and to enable
peer-to-peer learning. It is essential that you arrive prepared. We move fast, so there is no
second chance to have a productive classroom experience.
We require Piazza for asynchronous communication, available here. Main reasons:
1)​ Piazza enables students to answer each other’s questions. Peer answers tend to be fast
and accurate, and therefore encourage efficiency and fairness. Answers can inform
other students, and help to ensure that everyone can reference the same information.
2)​ Piazza enables private messages to ‘All Instructors’ for personal situations such as
illness. This is the place to notify us in advance if you have to miss class for a
university-sanctioned reason. Messages must be addressed to All Instructors so the
Professor & IA can coordinate and share records.
By default, any Piazza message should be visible to classmates, unless it contains personal or
confidential information. You can post anonymously to classmates if you prefer, but we
encourage use of names as the default. If you are posting confidential or private information,
please post it to ‘All Instructors’ so the professor and IA can both access it.
We discourage use of emails and canvas/instructure messages, as they forego most Piazza
benefits. We kindly ask you to note our communication preference, and in return we promise to
answer Piazza messages faster than any other asynchronous medium. If you email us, we will
ask you to kindly post your message to Piazza before we respond.
