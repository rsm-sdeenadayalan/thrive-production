# MGTA 415 — Analyzing Unstructured Data (NLP & LLM Applications)

**4 units** · MSBA (Rady MS Business Analytics) · offered SU25

## What this course covers

An applied course on natural language processing and large language models for business.
Briefly reviews classical NLP (preprocessing, embeddings) then moves into transformers,
prompt engineering, RAG, LLM agents, and multimodal models, with one comprehensive group
project building an AI-driven business application.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- applying NLP to business text data
- prompt engineering
- building RAG pipelines
- building LLM agent applications
- presenting analytical projects

## Topics covered

NLP and LLM fundamentals, embeddings, transformers, prompt engineering, LLM agents, retrieval-augmented generation, sentiment analysis, multimodal models, responsible AI.

## Tools used

Python, Hugging Face, NLTK, OpenAI API, LangChain, CrewAI, Pinecone, FAISS.

## Prerequisites

Math and basic statistics, Python programming, basic supervised machine learning.

## How it is graded

30% attendance/quizzes/labs, 30% midterm project presentation, 40% final project presentation.

## Workload

Moderate. One comprehensive group project with midterm and final presentations, plus quizzes and labs; no exams.

Overview
This course offers a comprehensive exploration of natural language processing (NLP) and large
language models (LLM), with a focus on applying these technologies to real-world business problems.
Over five weeks, we'll quickly review classical NLP techniques such as text preprocessing and
embeddings, then move rapidly into advanced LLM applications, including transformers, prompt
engineering, multimodal models, and agent-based AI interactions. This journey equips students with
foundational skills and state-of-the-art methodologies needed to transform unstructured text into valuable
business insights.
The course emphasizes high-level understanding of key NLP concepts through real-world
applications, interactive demos, and hands-on lab sessions. Specifically, students will work extensively on
one comprehensive group project, presenting their progress in a midterm session and delivering a final
presentation at the end of the course.
By the end of this course, students will have a strong understanding of advanced NLP and LLM
applications, enabling them to leverage these technologies effectively across diverse business scenarios,
significantly enhancing their analytical capabilities and strategic impact.

COURSE POLICIES
Use of Generative AI
"AI should be used to augment human intelligence, helping us make better decisions and
enhancing our capabilities.” — Fei-Fei Li
In this course, you are expected to learn to work with generative AI. The baseline performance you will be
benchmarked against is what can be generated by ChatGPT without advanced instructions. We will
provide a baseline solution for all assignments, including the prompts used to generate these solutions.
If you choose not to use generative AI, you may find yourself at a disadvantage. This class is designed for
you to collaborate with generative AI to create and explore solutions beyond the baseline, and to
understand the reasoning behind different NLP techniques.
The course is structured to reward those who can effectively leverage generative AI to enhance their
understanding of LLM/Gen AI technology. Your ability to use these tools to their fullest potential will be a
key factor in your success.
