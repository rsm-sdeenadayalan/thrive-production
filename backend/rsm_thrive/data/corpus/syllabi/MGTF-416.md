# MGTF 416 — MQF Professional Seminar

**1 units** · Rady MQF (Master of Quantitative Finance) · offered WI26

## What this course covers

A required seminar series for Master of Finance students featuring distinguished leaders
from the financial industry. Students attend weekly talks, learn about career paths and
current market challenges, and submit team-written summaries reflecting on each session.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- summarizing seminar insights
- asking thoughtful questions
- reflecting on professional development
- working in teams
- engaging with industry speakers

## Topics covered

financial industry leadership, career paths in finance, current market challenges, professional skill development, executive insights, corporate strategy, financial services, networking.

## Tools used

canvas.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory based on attendance and submission of team summaries.

## Workload

Light. Weekly seminar attendance and team-based 1-page summaries submitted after each session.

DESCRIPTION
MGTF416 Professional Seminar is a required one-credit course that counts towards your MFIN degree
requirements. The Professional Seminar Series offers access to distinguished innovative leaders as well
as professional skill development opportunities.
Your grade for this course is determined by your attendance and participation in the sessions, along with
a 1-page summary of each class. Documenting your participation is an important part of your
academic record and to receive credit for the class you must attend every class. Students will
check in with Jessica and her helpers each class in order for their attendance to be recorded.
OBJECTIVES
This class will provide access to leaders in the financial industry so that students may learn about their
career paths, their current roles, their firm, and the challenges they face in today’s markets.
MATERIALS
None, but your full attention to the speaker is required. There is a strict no phones or laptops policy in
force during this class. We are inviting distinguished executives from the industry, and we will all pay
careful attention to their talk.
SCHEDULE
The class meets in Beyster Auditorium from 11:30-12:30pm on Wednesdays. Attendance is mandatory.
ASSIGNMENTS
Submit a 1-page summary of what your team learned from a session by Saturday after the session at
5pm. Summaries should be submitted via Canvas – there will be a 3-day submission window after each
seminar. This is a team-based assignment but make sure the submitted summary includes the names of
each member of the team.
Two potential questions you may answer to help structure your summary would be: a) What did you find
valuable about this seminar? (what did you learn, what surprised you), b) What would have made this
seminar more useful? (what questions do you still have, what needed further clarification).
