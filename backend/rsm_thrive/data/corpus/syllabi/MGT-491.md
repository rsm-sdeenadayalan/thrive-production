# MGT 491 — Investments

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered SP26, SP27

## What this course covers

A survey of the investment landscape covering asset classes, securities, and modern
portfolio theory. Students use an Interactive Brokers trading account to track self-
managed portfolios and then dive into security selection. The course aims to develop and
analyze investment strategies that maximize returns and minimize risks for medium and
long-term horizons.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- analyzing asset class returns and risks
- building diversified investment portfolios
- conducting security selection and due diligence
- performing fixed income analysis including duration and convexity
- valuing equities using dcf and relative valuation
- using derivatives for hedging and speculation
- evaluating market efficiency and behavioral biases
- constructing multi-factor portfolios

## Topics covered

asset classes, modern portfolio theory, capital asset pricing model, efficient market hypothesis, behavioral finance, fixed income, equity valuation, derivatives, international investing, alternative investments.

## Tools used

interactive brokers, mvo calculator, canvas.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

Course grade is based on homework and quizzes (20%), credit and equity projects (30%), final team report or exam (30%), and participation (20%).

COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course is a survey of the investment landscape. We will analyze the characteristics, returns and risks of a variety of asset classes and the
securities within them - common equity, preferred equity, corporate debt, sovereign debt, municipal debt and derivatives. We will employ modern
portfolio theory to build a passive, diversified investment portfolio. To augment the textbook, students will make extensive use of an Interactive
Brokers (IB) trading account to track self-managed portfolios. We will then abandon portfolio theory with a hands-on dive into security selection.
Our end goal will be to develop and analyze investment strategies that maximize returns and minimize risks for investors with medium and long-term
investment horizons. We will question market efficieny on a weekly basis and be rewarded for our due diligence.
