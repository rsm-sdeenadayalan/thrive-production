# MGT 407 — Marketing

Rady MBA · offered FA25 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

Marketing Management focuses on strategic and tactical marketing decision-making in
dynamic business environments. This case-based course uses real-world cases and
discussions to reveal frameworks for driving growth, building customer relationships,
and staying competitive. Students apply marketing concepts to real-world scenarios and
develop integrated marketing plans.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- analyzing market opportunities
- developing marketing strategies
- segmenting and targeting customers
- positioning products and brands
- evaluating marketing campaign effectiveness
- creating integrated marketing plans
- applying marketing concepts to business decisions
- measuring customer lifetime value

## Topics covered

marketing strategy framework, customer needs and jobs to be done, segmentation, targeting, and positioning, product life cycle, customer lifetime value, promotion and marketing communications, branding and brand architecture, pricing strategy and behavioral pricing, distribution and place, marketing plan development.

## Tools used

canvas, generative ai tools.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Grading is based on participation/attendance (40 points), weekly quizzes (40 points), and a group marketing plan presentation and paper (20 points), with letter grades from A to F.

## Workload

Heavy. Weekly quizzes, class participation, case preparation, and a group marketing plan presentation and paper.

DESCRIPTION

Marketing Management focuses on the strategic and tactical aspects of marketing decision-making in dynamic
business environments. Understanding this topic is essential for driving business growth, building strong customer

concepts and frameworks that can inform management thoughts and actions. Our collective experience and
expertise outweighs and overshadows the knowledge any one faculty member can impart. Case discussions, and the
full participation they require, is an integral component of this course.

Class Participation (I)
Class participation is earned by “attending” class by contributing your thoughts and questions to the various
discussions in class. In practice, great ideas that are not articulated (or are not articulated in a compelling way) are
ignored. Thus, participation is important to your continued professional development. Further, much of the value of
an MBA comes from the interchange with classmates who come from various industries and strategic contexts. The
benefits that each of you receive from this class will be directly enhanced by the mutual sharing of insights. Thus,
throughout the term, I will track the degree to which you contribute to discussion. Please know that quality of
contribution (meaningful insights that help us progress in our discussion of the topic) far outweighs quantity.
Weekley Quizzes (G)
You will be asked to recognize, apply, and explore the marketing strategies discussed in the readings and the cases.
To solidify the knowledge, you will complete group discussion quizzes that will ask you to do deeper analysis or take
managerial stances and think deeper about the readings. These are not meant to explicitly test whether you have read
that week’s readings, but to answer the questions fully, understanding the weekly readings is necessary. However,
when completing these quizzes, you should not use your notes and class materials unless specifically noted. You
should not use the wider internet unless specifically noted. We want you to learn and be able to apply this
knowledge; LLMs already know this stuff. Hopefully, these quizzes will keep you “on task” by solidifying the
knowledge and keep you learning and engaged throughout the quarter.
Marketing Plan Presentation and Paper (G)
The marketing plan paper and presentation is a demonstration of the culmination of knowledge and application. See
the schedule for due dates. Detailed assignment instructions will be posted in Canvas. You will submit these
assignments through Canvas prior to the start of the last class.

This policy outlines the appropriate use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the Marketing Management course,
ensuring that the technology enhances learning while preserving the integrity of individual thought and effort. The
policy applies to all enrolled students in the Executive MBA Marketing Management course.
Permitted Use of AI:
o Understanding and Research: Students are permitted to use AI tools to deepen their understanding of
course materials and assist with research related to course topics. AI can be utilized to summarize
readings, provide explanations of complex concepts, and generate ideas for project brainstorming.
o Explicitly Allowed Assignments: AI may be used in completing assignments only when explicitly stated in
the assignment instructions. These will be designed to specifically leverage AI capabilities in a way that
enhances the learning objectives of the task.
Prohibited Use of AI:
o General Assignments and Examinations: The use of generative AI is strictly prohibited in the completion
of regular assignments and during examinations. Assignments are crafted to assess individual
understanding and the ability to apply marketing concepts independently.

Discussion Contributions: Participation in course discussions, whether online or in-person, must be free
