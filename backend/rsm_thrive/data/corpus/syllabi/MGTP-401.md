# MGTP 401 — Professional Seminar: Current Topics

**1 units** · Rady MPAc (Accountancy) · offered FA26, SP26

## What this course covers

This course gives students a window into what it is like to be a tax accounting
professional in a multinational corporation. It provides a high-level explanation of tax
accounting and case law concepts through guest speakers who are senior executives and
through study of recent real-life situations affecting the tax accounting profession.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- preparing for the far section of the cpa exam
- applying accounting topics to real-world examples
- identifying earnings manipulation using beneish and sloan models
- accounting for intercorporate investments
- translating multinational financial statements
- accounting for share-based compensation
- accounting for nonprofits and government entities
- analyzing tax accounts in financial statements
- explaining deferred tax accounting concepts
- discussing recent tax case law

## Topics covered

revenue recognition, inventory cost flow, intercorporate investments, multinational translation, research and development, beneish and sloan models, share-based compensation, nonprofit accounting, governmental accounting, cpa exam preparation, tax accounting, multinational corporate taxation.

## Tools used

becker cpa review, canvas, wiley efficient learning.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Graded on a pass/fail basis based on attendance and participation, including practice quizzes and activities.

## Workload

Moderate. Weekly practice quizzes, homework from Canvas and Becker's Test Bank, and required participation in the equity valuation contest.

DESCRIPTION
Less than half of CPA candidates pass the FAR section of the CPA exam. The goal for this class is to
prepare you for the FAR section of the CPA exam. Candidates for this exam generally spend 100+ hours
on the numerous, complex topics. We will look at a few topics each meeting and do practice questions. I
will share test prep resources and strategies that candidates have used in the past to successfully get
through this challenging part of the exam.

This class is intended to give students a window into what it is like as a practical matter to be a tax
accounting professional in a multinational corporation. As part of this journey, you will be provided with a
high-level explanation of some tax accounting and case law concepts. A few career advice nuggets will
be provided along the way as well. This will be accomplished through a series of guest speakers who are
senior executives in their companies, as well as studying recent real-life situations affecting the tax
accounting profession. You should expect to spend approximately 2-4 hours outside of class time every
two weeks for the quarter depending on how much you want to get out of the materials. You will discover
when the materials are reread, some of which include documents from real-life legal proceedings, you will
