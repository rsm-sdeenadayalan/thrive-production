# MGTF 422 — Derivatives and Structured Finance

Rady MQF (Master of Quantitative Finance) · offered WI26 · _unit count not stated in the material supplied_

## What this course covers

A comprehensive study of forwards, futures, options, and swaps in equity, currency, and
fixed income markets, covering pricing and applications to risk management. Topics
include hedge parameters such as Delta and Gamma, binomial option pricing,
collateralized debt obligations, and other structured products. The course is taught
primarily through lectures with numerical examples.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- pricing derivative contracts using no-arbitrage arguments
- replicating derivative payoffs
- applying hedging strategies for risk management
- computing option hedge parameters such as delta and gamma
- valuing forwards, futures, swaps, and options
- using binomial option pricing models
- analyzing collateralized debt obligations and structured products

## Topics covered

forwards and futures, commodities, interest rate and currency swaps, options, black-scholes model, greeks, binomial option pricing, fixed income derivatives, structured finance, risk management.

COURSE DESCRIPTION

The course provides a comprehensive study of forwards, futures, options,
and swaps in equity, currency, and fixed income markets. The pricing of
these derivative contracts as well as their applications to risk management
are covered in detail. Hedge parameters for options such as Delta and
Gamma will be studied along with the binomial option pricing framework. In
addition, the course will cover material related to collateralized debt
obligations (CDOs) and other structured products. The course will consist
primarily of lectures with a generous supply of numerical examples to
reinforce important concepts.
LEARNING OBJECTIVES
At the end of this course, you will be able to:
• Understand the concept of No Arbitrage is the foundation for pricing
derivatives
• Understand how pricing derivatives arises from replicating their
payoffs
• Understand how hedging enables the risk-neutral pricing of
derivatives
• Understand how best to choose derivatives for risk management
