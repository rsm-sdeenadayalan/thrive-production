# MGT 417 — Current Business Law Issues

**4 units** · Rady MBA · offered SU26

## What this course covers

An overview of current issues in business law, covering how legal rules shape business
decisions, contracts, risk management, and dispute resolution. Students learn
substantive areas of business law through lectures, case discussions, and practical
group exercises.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- analyzing contract provisions
- applying business formation principles
- evaluating legal risk in business decisions
- understanding dispute resolution options
- identifying types of intellectual property
- applying agency principles
- managing liability exposure

## Topics covered

legal system and sources of authority, agency principles, accountant liability, employment liability, business formation and corporations, securities regulation, contract formation and enforcement, litigation and dispute resolution, risk management and negligence, intellectual property, data privacy.

## Tools used

canvas.

## Prerequisites

None stated in the syllabus.

## How it is graded

Midterm 25%, group work/presentation 25%, class participation 25%, and final exam 25%.

Heavy. Three group projects, a midterm, a final exam, and weekly class participation.

DESCRIPTION
This course is designed to provide you with an understanding of current issues in business law and
provide practical exercises that will help you evaluate how the law influences business decision-making.
You will learn the basics of how the law shapes businesses as well as how it can be leveraged in a
commercial context. Specifically, we will spend significant time looking at how the law regulates the
behavior of actors within a commercial context, significant issues with respect to contracts, how law can
be utilized to create and mitigate business risk, and how legal disputes are resolved. Students will gain
practical understanding of major substantive areas of business law.

Group Projects
There will be three significant group exercises. The group exercises will require both collaboration with
classmates as well as oral presentation regarding specific aspects of the project. Students will be
evaluated based on my evaluation as to whether you provided a meaningful contribution to the group
effort.
Group Project 1 will require students to collaborate for purposes of creating a business and addressing
specific problems and challenges the group’s business will face. The group will present solutions and a
strategy for the business.
Group Project 2 will require students to apply principles of risk management and dispute resolution to
navigate a unique business dispute. Each group will be provided specific objectives, develop a strategy
for managing risk, and deploy that strategy against another group with conflicting interests.
Group Project 3 will require you to research and apply intellectual property subject matter for purposes of
our discussion of intellectual property.
The strength of your individual contributions to group project work will be considered. I also reserve the
right to consider peer feedback in evaluating your group work. In other words, your grades for group work
depend on both your collective and individual contributions.

Class Participation and Preparation
Class participation comprises approximately 25% of the student’s grade. Participation is measured, in
part, by the student’s willingness to volunteer meaningful insights during class discussion or ask a
question that assists the understanding of other students. My expectation is every student will be familiar
with the assigned prepared for class. If there is a compelling reason why you are not prepared to discuss
the assigned material for class, you should let me know in advance.
Participation credit can also be earned by presenting for the class a news article or current event that is
relevant to the subject matter of the course. If you come across a recent news article, please email at
least twenty-four (24) hours before the beginning of class. If approved, the student will briefly summarize
and the news article and receive participation credit.
Note that each class session is an opportunity to participate in discussion and/or present a current event.
Given there are ten (10) class sessions, there are ample opportunities to ensure full credit toward your
final grade for class participation.
Group Work
In addition to class discussion, there will be two major group presentations during the course. Your
contributions to group project work will also be considered for purposes of your class participation grade.
