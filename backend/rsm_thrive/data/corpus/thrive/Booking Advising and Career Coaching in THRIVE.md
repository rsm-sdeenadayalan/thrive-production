# How do I contact Amber Hanna or Nelitza Morales, or book advising or career coaching?

The way to reach either of them is to book a time in THRIVE, on the **Appointments** tab. You do not need to email first to get a meeting.

**Who to contact.** **Amber Hanna** is the **Graduate Student Advisor** — academic questions: course enrolment, registration, degree requirements, plans of study, deadlines, and general advising. **Nelitza Morales** is the **Career Coach** — career questions: internships, recruiting, job search, resumes, LinkedIn, and interview preparation. If you are not sure which you need, start with Amber Hanna; academic advising will answer or point you to the right person.

**To book a time with either of them:**

1. Open **Appointments** in the left-hand navigation. The page is titled "Book time for advising".
2. Pick the person: the **Academic advising** card is Amber Hanna, the **Career coaching** card is Nelitza Morales. Choosing one opens the booking panel.
3. Pick a **day** from the day chips. Only the next five business days are published, so a quiet strip means the week is full rather than broken.
4. Pick a **time** from that day's open slots.
5. Fill in **"What do you want to talk about?"** — a sentence is plenty, and it helps them prepare.
6. Press **Confirm booking**.

Once confirmed, the appointment appears in your list of booked times, you get a calendar invitation by email, and a Zoom link is created for the meeting.

**By email instead.** Booking is usually faster, because you are choosing from times they have already opened. If you would rather write: Amber Hanna is amhanna@ucsd.edu and Nelitza Morales is nemorales@ucsd.edu.

# Who do I talk to about my resume, LinkedIn, internships, recruiting, or the job search?

**Nelitza Morales**, the MSBA **Career Coach**, is the person for all of these: resume feedback, LinkedIn, internships, recruiting, the job search, and interview preparation.

Book time with her on the **Appointments** tab in THRIVE: open **Appointments** in the left-hand navigation, choose the **Career coaching** card, pick a day and an open time, say what you want to talk about, and press **Confirm booking**. You will get a calendar invitation and a Zoom link by email.

You can also email her at nemorales@ucsd.edu, though booking is usually faster because you are picking from times she has already opened.

The wider UC San Diego Career Center has drop-in services and written guides as well, but for MSBA-specific coaching Nelitza Morales is your first stop.

# How do I change or cancel an appointment I booked?

Your booked appointments are listed on the **Appointments** tab, the same place you booked them. Each one has a **Cancel** control, which releases the time so another student can take it.

To move an appointment, cancel the one you have and then book the new time — the released slot goes straight back into the list of open times.
