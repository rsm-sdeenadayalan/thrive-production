# Joint Ed.D. Program (UCSD/CSUSM) Registration Fees

Last Updated: August 14, 2026 1:52:35 PM PDT

Find tuition and fees for the 2026–2027 academic year for UC San Diego's Joint Ed.D. Program (UCSD/CSUSM). All fees are subject to change.

**Please note:** The fees shown on this page apply only to continuing students in the ED81 Joint Ed.D. Program. This program is no longer accepting new students.

Prospective and newly admitted students should refer to the [UC LEAD Ed.D. in Educational Leadership program website](https://eds.ucsd.edu/graduate/doctoral/ucleadedd/index.html) for current program information.

Some or all instruction for all or part of Academic Year 2026–2027 may be delivered remotely. Tuition and fees have been set regardless of the method of instruction and will not be refunded in the event instruction occurs remotely for any part of the Academic Year. Figures for tuition represent currently approved amounts and may not be final. Actual tuition and fees are subject to change by the University of California as determined to be necessary or appropriate. Final approved tuition and fee levels may differ from the amounts presented.

Full payment of all fees is required to complete registration.

## Other Fees

-   **[UCGPC Systemwide Fee](https://www.ucgpc501c3.org/ucgpc-fee):** ($7.00 annually, effective Fall 2023) fee assessed $3.00 Fall quarter, $2.00 Winter quarter, $2.00 Spring quarter
-   **[International Student Visa Administration Fee](https://students.ucsd.edu/_files/registrar/international-student-visa-admin-fee-2026.pdf):** All first-time enrolled international first-year, transfer or graduate students will be assessed a one-time visa administration fee of $200.00 during their term.
-   **Health Insurance:** Health insurance is required as a condition of enrollment. Students are automatically enrolled in the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) however, students already covered by an insurance plan may qualify for a health fee waiver. Please see the [Student Health Insurance Plan](https://shwadmin.ucsd.edu/uc-ship/index.html) page for more information, including waiver criteria, submission process and deadline.
-   **Non-California Residents:**
    -   Nonresident Supplemental Tuition for graduate students who have advanced to doctoral candidacy by the beginning of the term is reduced to $0.00 for a maximum of three (3) consecutive years.
    -   See [Residence for Tuition Purposes](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/residence/index.html).
