# Career Coaches

## Courtney Miklusak

Senior Director, Career Coaching | FlexEvening & Executive MBA Coach

![](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/users/2163060/profile-picture-1664492315-600x600-center-top.jpg?v=1664492315)

Courtney is the Senior Director for Career Coaching and coaches the Flex MBA students.  Courtney works with all the Rady Career Coaches to best prepare our students for their own career management and development.  Prior to joining Rady, Courtney worked in law school career services for 17 years after spending 7 years in the practice of law in San Diego.  As a long-time career coach to law students and lawyers, she managed all aspects of the career services at California Western School of Law.  Along with career coaching, she led and oversaw the office and employer development, professional development, student and alumni programming, placement reporting and the solo practice incubator program.  As an attorney, Courtney practiced both civil litigation and transactional law, working in small and medium size private law firms in business, real estate, finance, construction and professional malpractice. Courtney holds a Bachelor of Arts with Honors from University of California, Santa Barbara and a Juris Doctor from California Western School of Law.  She also is a California licensed attorney.
