# Marketing Explorer

Location: United States

Step Into Different Roles. Step Toward Your Future.

This simulation lets you step into several marketing roles through quick, focused scenarios based on real workplace challenges. You’ll get a taste of:

Creative storytelling and campaign planningData-driven performance analysisCustomer insight and product messagingTech-enabled marketing strategies

Each mini-simulation is designed to be short but realistic, giving you a feel for the day-to-day work and decision-making in each role. Try them all, notice which ones feel natural, and start to see where your skills and interests could take you next.

This program is self-paced. It takes approximately **30-40 mins** to complete.

-   **Intro & Scenario**  
    Background context and your project team
-   **Creative Strategist**  
    -   Stakeholder Management
    -   Client Communication
    -   Adaptability
    -   Creative Strategy
    -   Design Thinking
-   **Performance Analyst**

-   **Customer & Product Marketer**  
    -   User Research
    -   Customer Research
-   **Digital Marketer**  
    -   CRM
    -   Digital Marketing
    -   Data Analysis
-   **Reflect On What You Learned**
