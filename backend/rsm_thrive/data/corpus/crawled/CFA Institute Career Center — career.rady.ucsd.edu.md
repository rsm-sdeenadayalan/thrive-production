# CFA Institute Career Center

Explore finance-focused job opportunities through the CFA Institute Career Center. This specialized job board connects candidates with employers across investment management, asset management, equity research, risk management, banking, wealth management, and other financial services sectors.

## Why Use This Resource?

-   Search for finance and investment-related opportunities
-   Identify employers actively recruiting finance talent
-   Explore roles across multiple areas of financial services
-   Discover opportunities aligned with CFA program interests and career paths
-   Access a job board designed specifically for finance professionals

## What You’ll Find

-   Investment Analyst and Equity Research positions
-   Asset Management and Portfolio Management roles
-   Risk Management opportunities
-   Investment Banking and Corporate Finance positions
-   Wealth Management and Financial Advisory careers
-   Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) roles
-   Internship, early-career, and experienced professional opportunities
-   Search filters by location, industry, job function, and experience level

## Who Should Use This Resource?

Students interested in:

-   Investment Management
-   Asset Management
-   Equity Research
-   Portfolio Management
-   Investment Banking
-   Risk Management
-   Wealth Management
-   Corporate Finance
-   Financial Services

## Accessing the Resource

Select **View Resource** to search current job postings, explore employer profiles, and filter opportunities based on your career interests, location preferences, and professional goals.

## Important Note

Some features or job postings may provide enhanced access or visibility for CFA Institute members and CFA Program candidates. Review individual postings and access requirements directly through the platform.
