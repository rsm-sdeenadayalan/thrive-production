# TritonPay

## TritonPay - Your Central Hub for Billing and Payments

To deliver a more modern and flexible billing experience, Student Financial Solutions transitioned TritonPay to a mobile-friendly payment platform hosted by Transact Payments. Students and authorized payers can now enjoy an intuitive interface, flexible payment options, recurring autopay, and international bank wire transfers — all designed to make managing your bill easier than ever.

Visit [tritonpay.ucsd.edu](https://tritonpay.ucsd.edu/) to access your account, and follow us on Instagram [@ucsdsfs](https://instagram.com/ucsdsfs) for updates and helpful tips. Need guidance? Check out our step-by-step [video tutorials](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/tritonpay/tutorials.html) to help you get started.
