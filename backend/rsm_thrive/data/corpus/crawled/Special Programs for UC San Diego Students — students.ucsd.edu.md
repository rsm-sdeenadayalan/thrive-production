## Before you decide:

-   Talk with your [college](https://students.ucsd.edu/sponsor/college-contacts/index.html) and [department](https://ucsd.edu/academics/academics-detail.html) advisors for more information.
-   Review [How to Transfer Courses](https://students.ucsd.edu/academics/enroll/special-enrollment/transfer-courses.html) to make sure the classes you take will transfer to UC San Diego.
-   Use the online student-transfer information resource, **[ASSIST](http://www.assist.org/web-assist/welcome.html),** when considering approved lower-division courses at California Community Colleges.
