# LinkedIn Skills on the Rise 2026: Consulting

Explore LinkedIn’s annual analysis of the fastest-growing skills in the consulting profession. Based on LinkedIn workforce and hiring data, this report highlights the capabilities consultants are increasingly developing and employers are seeking in a rapidly evolving business environment.

## Why Use This Resource?

-   Identify the skills most in demand across consulting careers
-   Understand how AI is transforming consulting work
-   Prioritize skill development for consulting recruiting
-   Benchmark your capabilities against industry trends
-   Prepare for consulting internships and full-time opportunities
