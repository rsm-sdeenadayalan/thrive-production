# Rady School of Management Registration Fees

Last Updated: August 10, 2026 2:26:58 PM PDT

Find tuition, fees, and charges for the academic year 2026–2027 for UC San Diego's Rady School of Management. All fees are subject to change.

Some or all of instruction for all or part of Academic Year 2026–2027 may be delivered remotely. Tuition and fees have been set regardless of the method of instruction and will not be refunded in the event instruction occurs remotely for any part of the Academic Year. Figures for tuition represent currently approved amounts and may not be final. Actual tuition and fees are subject to change by the University of California as determined to be necessary or appropriate. Final approved tuition and fee levels may differ from the amounts presented.

Full payment of all fees is required to complete registration.

-   **Health Insurance:** Health insurance is required as a condition of enrollment. Students are automatically enrolled in the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) however, students already covered by an insurance plan may qualify for a health fee waiver. Please see the [Student Health Insurance Plan](https://shwadmin.ucsd.edu/uc-ship/index.html) page for more information, including waiver criteria, submission process and deadline.
-   **Non-California Residents:**
    -   Nonresident Supplemental Tuition for graduate students who have advanced to doctoral candidacy by the beginning of the term is reduced to $0.00 for a maximum of three (3) consecutive years.
    -   See [Residence for Tuition Purposes](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/residence/index.html).

## VA Benefits and Self-Supporting Programs

If you are or will be using VA educational benefits to fully or partially fund your degree (i.e., Chapter 33 Post 9/11 GI Bill® or Chapter 31 Veterans Readiness and Employment (VRE)), the **tuition** and **registration fees** on this page marked "self-supporting programs" are eligible to be covered by the VA. Please note that the UCGPC Systemwide Fee and the International Student Visa Administration Fee are not eligible to be covered by VA Benefits. The UC Student Health Insurance Plan (UC SHIP) fee may be covered by the Chapter 33 Post 9/11 GI Bill, but cannot be covered by the Chapter 31 VRE program. For more information, visit the [**UCSD VA Benefits**](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/veterans/index.html) website.
