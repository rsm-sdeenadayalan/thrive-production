# Intro to AI for Financial Planning and Analysis

![Intro to AI for Financial Planning and Analysis](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/176/2025/05/9a5318107fe97ae0d5983d9b09717214-default-480x320-center-middle.jpg?v=123875)

Finance

Learn how AI speeds up FP&A work such as forecasting, scenario modelling and variance storytelling while keeping every output accurate, auditable and compliant.

### About the course

Financial planning and analysis teams need both speed and accuracy. This course shows how to use AI to draft forecasts, build scenario models, and turn numbers into clear narratives for business leaders. You will learn when AI helps, where it must be limited, and how to maintain compliance and audit trails. The course covers verification steps, human approval before release, and full evidence retention from prompt to final version. Regional standards are addressed, including SOX 404 internal control, SEC Regulation FD for forward-looking language, and GDPR for people related cost data. Whether you are an analyst or a manager, you will learn to partner with AI while keeping quality, trust, and control intact.

### Skills you’ll gain

-   Use AI for forecasting and variance storytelling
-   Generate driver-based forecasts scenarios with AI
-   Translate financial data into clear and actionable narratives
-   Apply responsible AI practices in financial modelling
-   Implement human approval and evidence retention controls

This course is 27 mins long.
