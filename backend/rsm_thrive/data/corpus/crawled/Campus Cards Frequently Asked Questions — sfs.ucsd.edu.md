# Campus Cards Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need to replace my student ID card because of the transition to the Triton Student System (TSS)?

As part of the transition to the new Triton Student System (TSS), student PIDs are being replaced with Triton Student Numbers (TSNs).

**You do not need to replace your current student ID card.** Your existing ID card will continue to work normally, even if it displays your PID.

If you request a replacement ID card for another reason (such as a lost, wear/tear, or name change card), your new ID card will display your TSN instead of your PID. Replacing your ID card solely to receive a TSN is not necessary.

## How do I get my ID Card? (Updated for Fall 2026)

**Incoming Fall 2026 Students**

Incoming undergraduate and graduate students should submit their ID card photo through the **Online Photo Tool**. Once your photo has been approved, your ID card will be printed and distributed according to your student status (for example, at your move-in location, through your graduate program, or during the Campus ID Card Distribution Event). No ID Card Request Form is required for your first ID card.

**Continuing Students and Replacement ID Cards**

Continuing students requesting a replacement ID card should complete the [ID Card Request Form](https://support.ucsd.edu/students?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=4cafd07e1b941190484f968f034bcb7b). Once your request has been reviewed by our staff, you will receive an email with pickup instructions when your ID card is ready.

Please note that the Campus Cards Office is unable to mail new or replacement ID cards.

Before an ID card can be issued, students must have an approved photo on file through the online [Photo Tool](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/campus-cards/photo-tool.html).

## I lost my card, does the Campus Cards office have it?

The Campus Cards Office will contact you via your UCSD email address if your ID card has been turned into our office. If you do not receive an email from us, then we do not have your ID card.

If you are told by another office or department that your ID card was sent to our office, but our office has not received it, the ID card could still be in transit via the inter-campus mail system. Unless you hear directly from our office that we have received your ID card, unfortunately we do not have it.

## What if I prefer to use my Lived Name?

Campus ID cards are printed with your legal name, unless a lived name is provided prior to the ID card being printed.  If you prefer to use your lived name, you can submit a _**lived name**_ on the **[Social Identities Tool](https://act.ucsd.edu/personalinfo/)** via [**My TritonLink**](https://students.ucsd.edu/my-tritonlink/tools/ "https://students.ucsd.edu/my-tritonlink/tools/"). The Social Identity Tool allows students to set their lived name and lived personal pronouns in University records and systems.

-   If you need a replacement ID card with your lived name, please submit an ID Card request form. For a limited time, there is no charge for the **first** replacement ID card due to a lived name change. Subsequent replacement ID cards will cost $25.00.
-   Please allow 1-2 business days (after you submit your update to the Social Identities Tool) for our systems to be updated with your lived name before requesting an ID card.
-   Please note if you are using a lived name significantly different from your legal name you will want to make sure that the lived name is printed on your Campus ID card and that you have that ID card available to you when you are doing an activity where your identity must be verified by the university such as taking a proctored exam.
