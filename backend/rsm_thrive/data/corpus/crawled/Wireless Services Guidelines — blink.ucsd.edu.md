# Wireless Services Guidelines

Last Updated: October 25, 2024 9:25:14 AM PDT

Read about UCSD's wireless services guidelines.

UC San Diego has wireless guidelines pertaining especially to public areas where several campus entities have an interest in similar wireless services.

-   **General rules:** Wireless services are subject to the same rules and standards that govern other communications services at UCSD. In particular, section VIB-E of Policies and Procedures Manual [section 135-3](http://adminrecords.ucsd.edu/ppm/docs/135-3.html) apply to wireless equipment and must be followed.
-   **Radio interference:** Because the wireless network is a system that transmits radio signals and is a shared resource, it's subject to [radio communication rules](https://policy.ucop.edu/doc/7020448/BFB-IS-5) (PDF) concerning interference and shared use.
-   **Reporting:** All wireless equipment must be approved by and [registered](https://netapps-web.ucsd.edu/secure/etherreg/wirelessform.pl) with IT Services.
-   **(802.11a/b/g/n) Network Standards:** To successfully deploy an 802.11a/b/g network, all equipment operating in the 2.4 and 5 GHz spectrum must be carefully installed and configured in coordination with IT Services.
