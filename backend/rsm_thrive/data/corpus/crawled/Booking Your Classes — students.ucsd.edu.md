Find information and links for booking your class schedule at UC San Diego.

## Important news

Log into [WebReg](https://act.ucsd.edu/webreg2) to manage your Summer Session 2026 courses.

**Log into TSS ([sis.ucs.edu](https://sis.ucsd.edu/)) to find available classes and to book your courses for Fall 2026.** For details, see the [how-to pages linked here](https://students.ucsd.edu/my-tritonlink/tools/tool-help/index.html), and [TSS Student Info](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1ETRVFSF0tI0dSB_gLdaUuAqTeSqOodYYOkDmBhnmh-k/edit?slide=id.g396fb806eaa_0_46#slide=id.g396fb806eaa_0_46).
