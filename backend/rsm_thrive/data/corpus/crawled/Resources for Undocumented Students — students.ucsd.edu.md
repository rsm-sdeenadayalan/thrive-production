# Resources for Undocumented Students

Last Updated: May 15, 2026 3:54:05 PM PDT

Link to AB 540 and undocumented student resources.

As part of the California Education Code, AB 540 allows certain students who have attended high school in California for 3 or more years and earned a high school diploma or its equivalent to be exempt from paying nonresident tuition at California public universities, including UC San Diego.

## Campus resources

-   [Financial Aid & Scholarships Office](https://fas.ucsd.edu/): Learn about the [California Dream Act and how to apply](https://fas.ucsd.edu/applying/financial-aid-application-tips/ca-dream-act-tips.html); and about [private scholarships that may be available to noncitizens and undocumented students](https://fas.ucsd.edu/types/scholarships/outside-agency-scholarships.html#private-scholarships).
    
-   [Student Legal Services](https://sls.ucsd.edu/): Obtain free, confidential counseling and education on all types of legal matters, including AB 540 issues and/or undocumented status.
    
-   [UC San Diego Cross-Cultural Center](https://ccc.ucsd.edu/): Maintains a list of links offering information and assistance to AB 540 and undocumented students.
    
-   [University of California Undocumented Student Resources](https://undoc.universityofcalifornia.edu/): Includes information about applying to UC, legislation basics, and links to systemwide resources.
    
-   [AB 540 University of California](https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/tuition-financial-aid/tuition-cost-of-attendance/ab-540-nonresident-tuition-exemption.html): Offers a wide variety of information for AB 540 students, families, and advisors (in both English and Spanish).

## Other

-   [AB 540 CSU Resources](https://www.calstate.edu/attend/student-services/resources-for-undocumented-students/Pages/default.aspx): California State University provides application information, resource links, and answers to frequently asked questions from AB 540 students.
    
-   [American Immigration Lawyers Association (AILA)](https://www.aila.org/): A national association that provides legal services while advocating for fair immigration laws and policies. AILA offers licensed, professional, and free and low-cost help, and resources for researching immigration laws.
    
-   [Ayuda Financiara en Español](https://www.usa.gov/espanol/ayuda-financiera-para-estudiantes): Financial aid information and links in Spanish.
    
-   [Beca Foundation](https://www.becafoundation.org/): A nonprofit Latino scholarship organization that provides options for Latino students seeking to fund their pursuit of higher education.
    
-   [Border Angels](https://www.borderangels.org/): A local grassroots organization that protects the rights of immigrants.
    
-   [California Immigrant Policy Center](https://www.caimmigrant.org/): Promotes reasonable immigration policies and informs the public on the latest developments on immigration law and policy and their impacts on the public.
    
-   [The Coalition for Humane Immigrant Rights of Los Angeles](https://www.chirla.org/): Offers information on immigration rights and advocacy.
    
-   [DACA and Your Workplace Rights FAQ](https://www.nilc.org/work/workers-rights/): A list of frequently asked questions about employment rights of DACA-approved individuals, prepared by the National Immigration Law Center.
    
-   [The Dream](https://www.thedream.us/): The website for a national campaign to support DREAMers, with news, information, and helpful links.
    
-   [Immigrants Rising](https://immigrantsrising.org/): Supporting students and the right to transform lives through education.
    
-   [Latino College Dollars](https://latinocollegedollars.org/): Offers information on scholarships for Latino students, compiled by the Tomás Rivera Policy Institute.
-   [Legal Aid Society of San Diego](https://www.lassd.org/): Provides assistance to low-income individuals and disadvantaged communities for a variety of legal matters, including immigration issues.
    
-   [Mexican American Legal Defense and Education Fund](https://www.maldef.org/education/): Features policy analysis and eligibility requirements for AB 540 students.
    
-   [National Immigration Law Center](https://www.nilc.org/): Offers information on the Federal DREAM Act, in addition to other resources on immigration law and policy.
    
-   [San Diego Immigrants & New Americans](https://www.immigrantsandiego.org/): This San Diego-based organization focuses on immigration reform, with an emphasis on educating the local immigrant community about its rights.
