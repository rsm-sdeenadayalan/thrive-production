# PNC: Key Roles and Business Segments

Location: United States

Learn how roles across PNC connect to deliver value for customers, communities, and the business.

Banks like PNC don’t run on money alone — they run on people organized around clients, services, and skills. This program gives you a clear look at how financial services are structured, from business segments that focus on different types of customers, to job families that group roles by shared strengths and work styles.

In this program, you’ll:

Learn about PNC’s business segments and how they support individuals, businesses, and communities.Explore broad job families — customer-facing, analytical, tech-driven, and operations — to see how different kinds of work connect.Practice reflecting on your own skills and interests, a useful exercise for any career path.Build confidence in making informed choices about classes, activities, or future opportunities.

By the end, you’ll have a big-picture understanding of how PNC organizes work, plus a stronger sense of how to match your strengths to different types of roles — an ability that will serve you well in any workplace.

This program is self-paced. It takes approximately **30-60 mins** to complete.

-   **Intro & Scenario**  
    Background context and your project team
-   **PNC’s business segments**  
    Learn about the core segments and the kinds of customers they serve
    -   Client Services
    -   Product Expertise
    -   Team Collaboration
    -   Business Acumen
-   **Career paths at PNC**  
    Explore specific jobs and job “families”
    -   Career Planning
    -   Skills Assessment
    -   Strengths Assessment
-   **Finish Line**
