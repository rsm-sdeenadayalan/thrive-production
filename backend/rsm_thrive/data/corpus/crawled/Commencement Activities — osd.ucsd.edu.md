# Commencement Activities

## Commencement Activities Information

UC San Diego welcomes people with disabilities in our commencement ceremonies and celebrations by providing physical and program accessibility. For specific accommodations, please contact your [student's college](https://students.ucsd.edu/academics/commencement/index.html "Commencement").
