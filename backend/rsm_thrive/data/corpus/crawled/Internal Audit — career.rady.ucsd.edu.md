# Internal Audit

Location: United States

-   Share This:

Explore the world of Internal Audit with the team at Goldman Sachs

At Goldman Sachs, we believe who you are makes you better at what you do. For us, it’s all about bringing together people who are curious, collaborative, and driven to make things possible for our clients and communities.

In this program, you’ll step into the role of a Goldman Sachs employee and delve into the intricate financial operations of one of the world’s leading investment banks.

By participating in this virtual job simulation, you’ll gain firsthand experience in the world of accounting at Goldman Sachs and discover the critical role auditors play in the financial ecosystem. Whether you’re passionate about numbers or aspire to contribute to the financial stability of global institutions, this program is your opportunity to explore the exciting career prospects that await you in accounting at one of the world’s most respected investment banks.

Interested? Participate in our Internal Audit Job Simulation to learn more!

This program is self-paced. It takes approximately **30-60 mins** to complete.

## Skills Learned:

-   **Foundations of Internal Audit**  
    Discover the fundamentals of internal auditing by applying them to everyday life decisions
    -   Audit Procedures
    -   Risk Management
    -   Critical Thinking
    -   Risk Assessment
-   **Risk Assessment in Global Banking & Markets**  
    Evaluate risk scenarios based on audit findings from the Global Banking and Markets team
    -   Risk Identification
    -   Risk Assessment
    -   Analytical Thinking
    -   Auditing
-   **Finish Line**
