# ARCS Scholars at UC San Diego

We are pleased to announce the

[San Diego Chapter of the ARCS Foundation](https://san-diego.arcsfoundation.org/)

will fund a total of

26 new and returning scholars at $12,500 per scholar for this academic year.

See the [2025-2026 ARCS Scholars here](https://san-diego.arcsfoundation.org/scholars/2025-2026-arcs-scholars) and the [2024-2025 Scholars here](https://san-diego.arcsfoundation.org/scholars/2024-2025-arcs-scholars).
