# How to Fulfill the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement

Last Updated: June 10, 2026 5:34:10 PM PDT

Find out how to meet the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement.

## What is the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR)?

-   The University of California requires all undergraduate students to demonstrate a minimum proficiency in English composition by meeting the Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR). This is one of the [undergraduate degree requirements](https://ucsd.edu/catalog/front/UgrdDegReq.html).
-   You must fulfill the ELWR before you are able to enroll in university-level writing courses.
-   You must fulfill the ELWR within a limited time frame.

## How do I meet the ELWR?

Prior to enrollment at UC San Diego, you may meet the requirement with a qualifying SAT, ACT, AP, or IB test score, or a transferable college composition course. Visit the [ELWR page](https://awp.ucsd.edu/elwr/index.html) for details.

## What if I don't have a qualifying score or course?

-   You must complete the [Writing Placement Process](https://awp.ucsd.edu/placement/index.html) (WPP) in summer.
-   If the WPP places you in College Writing courses, you have fulfilled the ELWR.
-   If the WPP places you in AWP 3 or AWP 4A-4B, you must enroll in the course you are placed into during the first quarter it's offered at UC San Diego. You will fulfill the ELWR when you pass AWP 3 or AWP 4B with a C or better.
