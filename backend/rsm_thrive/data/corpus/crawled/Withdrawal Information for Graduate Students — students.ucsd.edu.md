# Withdrawal Information for Graduate Students

Last Updated: November 24, 2025 12:18:42 PM PST

Give [feedback](https://eforms.ucsd.edu/view.php?id=339417)

If you are a graduate student who plans to withdraw from UCSD:

-   Contact your [graduate program](http://blink.ucsd.edu/sponsor/academic/index.html).
-   If your fees are fully paid by financial aid, graduate support, or other programs, you must make contact by the Friday of Week 1.
-   Read more about [withdrawal information for financial aid and loan recipients](https://fas.ucsd.edu/forms-and-resources/withdrawing/index.html).

#### Questions? Contact the appropriate resource:

-   [Your graduate program](http://blink.ucsd.edu/sponsor/academic/index.html)
-   [Graduate Division](http://grad.ucsd.edu/)
-   [Financial Aid & Scholarships Office](https://fas.ucsd.edu/)

**International Students:** Contact the [International Student Office](http://icenter.ucsd.edu/ispo/), (858) 534-3730 before you withdraw.
