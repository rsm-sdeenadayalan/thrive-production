## National DeafBlind Equipment Distribution Program

The National DeafBlind Equipment Distribution Program (NDBEDP) offers free equipment and training to low-income DeafBlind and Hard-of-Hearing/Low-Vision individuals, with the goal of supporting long-distance communication. The NDBEDP provides phones, tablets, computers, videophones, and similar items with adaptive software. Participants receive training in their preferred language modality until they are comfortable using their devices. To learn more, visit: [www.icanconnect.org](http://www.icanconnect.org/)

## NextGen Leaders Initiatives

NextGen Leaders are college students and recent graduates with disabilities who have demonstrated talent and leadership in the STEM, finance and business fields. NextGen Leaders collaborate with Corporate Partners to prepare for employment through mentorship, networking and recruiting opportunities. For more information, visit: [disabilityin.org/what-we-do/nextgen-leaders-initiatives](https://disabilityin.org/what-we-do/nextgen-leaders-initiatives/)

## San Diego Ducks Sled Hockey

### What they do

-   mentor and coach players of all ages and abilities in sled hockey - no experience necessary!
-   train players to advance to their desired level of competition, from recreational to international
-   offer local, regional, and national competition
-   host clinics and learn to play events
-   provide free equipment lending with sleds, sticks, and protective equipment
-   assist players with fundraising and grant opportunities
-   provide service opportunities for the players to give back to the community
-   offer volunteer opportunities for those who wish to join the staff
-   uphold USA Hockey, SafeSport, and Paralympic Sport Club requirements
-   represent the NHL Anaheim Ducks as their affiliate sled hockey teams
-   maintain 501(c)(3) nonprofit status as a fiscally sponsored project of Mission Edge

For more information, visit [www.duckssledhockey.org](https://www.duckssledhockey.org/ "Link to San Diego Ducks Sled Hockey website").

## UC System Disability Ad Hoc Committee

To learn more, visit [the committee's website](https://dsp.sa.ucsb.edu/student-resources/career-resources-and-opportunities-dsp-students/uc-system-disability-ad-hoc).
