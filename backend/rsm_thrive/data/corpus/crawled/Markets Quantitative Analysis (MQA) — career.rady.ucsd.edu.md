# Markets Quantitative Analysis (MQA)

Location: United States

Master financial markets with Citi’s immersive Quantitative Analyst Experience

Welcome to the Citi Markets Quant Experience! We are thrilled to have you here.

At Citi, we are a global bank committed to enabling progress and economic growth. With a legacy spanning over two centuries, we empower individuals, businesses, governments, and communities to achieve their financial goals.

During this program, you will get the opportunity to step into the shoes of a quantitative analyst and complete tasks that replicate the work that our Markets Quant team does every day. You’ll learn key skills in financial risk management, securities structuring, quantitative analysis, and market research.

We hope this program provides a great resource for you to upskill and strengthen your resume as you explore career options and a potential career at Citi.

This program is self-paced. It takes approximately **3-4 hours** to complete.

-   **Intro & Scenario**  
    Background context and your project team
-   **Financial math fundamentals and team structure**  
    Gain a solid mathematical foundation and insight into Citi’s Markets Quant team structure for success in financial analysis.
    -   Capital Markets
    -   Collaboration
    -   Teamwork
    -   Critical Thinking
    -   Financial Mathematics
-   **Pricing commodities – coffee**  
    Navigate the coffee futures market: Strategize, analyze, and price with precision
    -   Market Analysis
    -   Quantitative Analysis
    -   Critical Thinking
    -   Communication Skills
-   **Hedging and structuring securities**  
    Craft tailored financial solutions to navigate market fluctuations and optimize performance.
    -   Financial Analysis
    -   Risk Management
    -   Quantitative Analysis
    -   Communication
    -   Customer Focus
-   **Managing risk across market trade units**  
    Navigate market turbulence and optimize performance with expert risk management strategies in Citi’s dynamic financial markets
    -   Risk Assessment
    -   Risk Strategy
    -   Quantitative Analysis
