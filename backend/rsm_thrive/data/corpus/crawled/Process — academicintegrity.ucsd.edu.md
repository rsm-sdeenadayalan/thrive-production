# Process

## Resolving an Allegation

Being alleged with an academic integrity violation can be overwhelming and confusing, so we want to help.

## Helpful Resources

You can find helpful information by navigating through different sections of this webpage, including:

-   [UC San Diego Academic Integrity Policy and Procedures](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/policy.html)
-   [What happens when cheating is suspected](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/after-report.html)
-   [Your rights as a student](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/students-rights.html)
-   [Where you can get help](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/help.html) 
-   [The consequences of cheating](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/consequences/index.html) 
-   [Academic Integrity Reviews](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/airb/index.html)
-   [Appeal Options](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/process/appeals/index.html)
-   [A form to request your academic integrity case documentation](https://academicintegrity.ucsd.edu/_archive/form-ai-doc-request.html)

If you would like a Student Advocate to review your case and advise you throughout the process, you can make an appointment with the [Office of Student Advocacy](https://asadvocacy.ucsd.edu/).
