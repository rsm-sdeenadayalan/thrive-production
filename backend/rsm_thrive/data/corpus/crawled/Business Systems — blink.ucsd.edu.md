# Business Systems

Last Updated: August 28, 2024 2:55:10 PM PDT

Give [feedback](https://eforms.ucsd.edu/view.php?id=252329/)

Learn about business systems accounts at UCSD.

To register as a new user, first read [How to Get Access to UCSD Business Systems (Support Portal)](https://support.ucsd.edu/services?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010510).

**Note:**

-   Some business systems require prior authorization/ training.
-   See [Business Systems/ Link Family](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/help-desk/applications/link-family/BS/index.html) for a complete list.
-   You must use a [different password](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/network/access/index.html) for campus email and network/ departmental systems.
-   You will need a valid [UCSD email address](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/tasks/get-address.html) (abcd@ucsd.edu, not abcd@dept.ucsd.edu) and an active, paid appointment to register a new business systems account.

Contact your [Department Security Administrator (DSA)](https://iam.ucsd.edu/dsasearch/dsaSearchByEmail) or the [ITS Service Desk](https://support.ucsd.edu/its), (858) 246-4357 or ext. 6-HELP.
