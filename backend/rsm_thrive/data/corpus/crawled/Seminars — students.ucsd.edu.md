# Seminars

Last Updated: July 13, 2026 5:50:16 PM PDT

Find links to information about 1-unit seminars for seniors and first-year students at UC San Diego.

## First-year 87 seminars

First-year 87 seminars are small, informal classes (20-student limit) that are made up of other first-year students and a few sophomores.

-   [Read more about first-year seminars](http://ugseminars.ucsd.edu/freshmen.aspx)
-   [See this quarter's list of First-Year 97 seminars](http://ugseminars.ucsd.edu/Listing.aspx), and learn how to enroll

## Senior 192 seminars

Senior 192 seminars are small classes that give upper-division students the opportunity to strengthen relationships with faculty members.

-   [Read more about senior seminars](http://ugseminars.ucsd.edu/Senior.aspx)
-   [See this quarter's list of Senior 192 seminars](http://ugseminars.ucsd.edu/Listing.aspx?c=s), and learn how to enroll
