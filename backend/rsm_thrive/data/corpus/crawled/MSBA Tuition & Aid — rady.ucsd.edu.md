# MSBA Tuition & Aid

## Tuition & Aid: M.S. in Business Analytics

Your Rady MS in Business Analytics is an investment of time, energy and money. Early consideration of how you will finance your studies ensures that you will be ready and able to accept an offer of admission. The Rady School is committed to helping you identify your options and providing advice to assist you with your financial decisions.

## Tuition and Program Fees

Master of Science in Business Analytics academic fees can be found on the [TritonLink page](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/registration/2025-26/rady.html#master-of-quantitative-finance-self-supported-prog "https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/registration/2025-26/rady.html#master-of-quantitative-finance-self-supported-prog"). Fees are subject to change. The Rady Master Science in Business Analytics program is a 11-month program with a total of 50 credit units. The fees are billed quarterly. Fees do not include student health insurance and other campus fees, books and course materials, or living expenses.

## Scholarships

Rady provides limited scholarship support to exceptional individuals. All domestic and international admitted students are considered for merit-based scholarship upon admission. No additional application is required. [Click here for more information about our donor-supported and other special fellowships](https://rady.ucsd.edu/programs/fellowships/index.html). All graduates of any University of California campus are eligible for the Rady-funded UC Scholarship in the amount of $10,000.

## Loans

To be considered for financial assistance or non-subsidized loans, students should complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). To receive priority funding, file your FAFSA as early as Jan. 1, but no later than March 2. Complete and submit your [FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa). UC San Diego’s school code is 001317. Once your FAFSA data has been processed and transferred to the UC San Diego's Financial Aid Office by the FAFSA central processor, you will be notified of your eligibility by the Financial Aid Office.

[UC San Diego's Financial Aid Office](https://fas.ucsd.edu/index.html) provides step-by-step application directions, downloadable forms, answers to frequently asked questions and more.

U.S. citizens or permanent residents should apply for Federal Stafford Loans first because of favorable interest rates and origination fees. These are variable, low-interest educational loans. The Federal Stafford Loan Program provides unsubsidized loans where you pay accruing interest while in school, grace and authorized deferment periods. These loans are need-based. They do not require a co-signer and are not based on your credit history. Deferment options are available. The FAFSA is required to determine what loans may be available.

Private educational loans are available to domestic and international applicants. For international applicants, some lending institutions may require that a U.S. citizen co-sign.

## Student Contribution Appeal

Your expected student contribution for the academic year will be based largely on your prior calendar-year income. For students who have been working full-time during the prior year, this expected contribution may not accurately reflect your current or projected financial situation as a full-time student. Students falling into this situation should submit a [student contribution appeal form](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/financial-aid/applying/appeals/).

## VA Benefits and Self-Supporting Programs

If you are or will be using VA educational benefits to fully or partially fund your degree (i.e. _Chapter 33 Post 9/11 Gi Bill®_ or _Chapter 31 Veterans Readiness and Employment (VRE)_), all **tuition** and **registration fees** on this page are eligible to be covered by the VA. Please note that the UCGPC Systemwide Fee and the International Student Visa Administration Fee are not eligible to be covered by VA Benefits. The UC Student Health Insurance Plan (UC SHIP) fee may be covered by the Chapter 33 Post 9/11 GI Bill, but cannot be covered by the Chapter 31 VRE program. For more information, visit the [**UCSD VA Benefits**](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/registration/2025-26/self-supporting.html#https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/veterans/index.html "https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/registration/2025-26/self-supporting.html#https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/veterans/index.html") website.
