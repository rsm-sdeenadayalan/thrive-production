# How to Wait-List a Class

Last Updated: August 18, 2026 3:33:40 PM PDT

If you want to book a class that is full, you can put yourself on a wait list.

## Facts about wait-listed classes:

-   Although the two-pass booking process does not apply to graduate students, wait-listing is not available to students until undergraduates complete their first booking window.
-   Classes can be wait-listed until 4:30 p.m. on Thursday of the second full week of classes. See [deadlines](https://blink.ucsd.edu/instructors/courses/enrollment/calendars/) for the academic year.
-   If you don't meet class requirements when you attempt to move from the wait list into the class, you will not be added to the class.
-   You may request authorization to book the course through [Triton Enrollment Authorization (TEA)](https://students.ucsd.edu/my-tritonlink/tools/tool-help/tea.html), but cannot request to be added to a wait list.
-   The last run of the auto wait-list program is Thursday of the second full week of classes at 10 p.m.

## About wait lists in the new Triton Student System (TSS):

-   In TSS, waitlist requests run continuously, instead of just once a day. As soon as a seat becomes available in a course in TSS, the first student on the waitlist is immediately added to the course in real time — there's no need to keep checking back.
-   The status on your Student Overview Homepage will change from waitlisted (shown as **Booked on Wait List**) to **Booked** once you're in.
-   **Be careful when dropping a course!** If you drop a course and the course has a waitlist, you'll need to join the waitlist and wait for your turn to be re-added to the course.
-   If you need to switch a course section, use the **Switch Section** option in TSS instead of dropping the course and re-adding. Note that the Switch Section action will release your seat from the previous course to an individual on the waitlist, if a waitlist for the course exists.
-   While you're on the waitlist, you have the same options as a booked student:
    -   **Drop** — removes you from the waitlist
    -   **Switch** — moves you to another waitlist, or to a section of the same course that has open seats
-   View [more information and instructions on waitlisting](https://students.ucsd.edu/my-tritonlink/tools/tool-help/booking.html#waitlists).

## If the class or section you want is full:

-   [Triton Student System (TSS)](https://sis.ucsd.edu/) will offer you the option of booking another open section of that class, or wait-listing the section.
-   You can only wait-list for one section of a course.
-   You can't be simultaneously booked and wait-listed in different sections of a course.
-   Use TSS if you are pre-authorized to book a full class.

**Note:** Classes you have wait-listed will display in TSS.

## If you change your mind and want to add or wait-list a different section of a class for which you are already booked:

-   If you need to switch a course section, use the **Switch Section** option in TSS instead of dropping the course and re-adding. Note that the Switch Section action will release your seat from the previous course to an individual on the waitlist, if a waitlist for the course exists.
-   You can't be simultaneously booked and wait-listed in different sections of a course.

## If you want to find out if you were placed into a section from the wait list:

-   If you're booked in a wait-listed class, you'll be notified by email and it will be included in your class list on TSS.
-   Confirm your grading option on [TSS](https://sis.ucsd.edu/).
