**Important**: Please do not attempt to use investment or brokerage accounts, as they will be rejected. If you using a savings account, confirm with your bank to verify if E-Check payments are allowed.

As a UCSD student, you can go to the **[Billing and Payment tool](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/billing-payment/index.html)** to make E-Check payments. You can also **[authorize a parent or other payer](https://youtu.be/FWKsXlM4KxM)** to make E-Check payments.

## SETUP YOUR PROFILE

You can store your account information as a payment profile so you won't have to re-enter it each time you use E-Check. To create a payment profile follow the steps in [this tutorial](https://youtu.be/uA6CGRqf9w8).

## PAY YOUR BILL

With E-Check, you can choose to pay any of these amounts:

-   **Last statement amount**, the amount shown on your last billing statement
-   **Summary Balance Levels,** sections of your bill organized into different categories, which can be paid in full or a custom amount.
-   **Current balance**, an updated account balance that includes your last statement amount and any adjustments since the last billing date.

* * *

### E-CHECK FEES

-   **$0.50 service fee** for each transaction, added to the payment amount deducted from your checking account.
-   **$25 returned e-check fee** for all returned transactions.

* * *

## SECURITY

E-Check uses the latest security technologies, including Secure Sockets Layer software, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and database level encryption technology. Information is stored on secure computers located in a biometrically locked data center.
