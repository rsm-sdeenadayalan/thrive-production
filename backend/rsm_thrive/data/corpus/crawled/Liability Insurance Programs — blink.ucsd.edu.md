# Liability Insurance Programs

Last Updated: February 18, 2026 11:14:39 AM PST

Learn about liability insurance programs available for UC San Diego departments.

## Types of insurance

More than 30 kinds of insurance are available to protect teaching, research, and public service activities from liability and financial loss due to:

-   Fire
-   Floods
-   Theft
-   Accidents
-   Legal liability
-   Other unexpected events

[Automatic programs](https://blink.ucsd.edu/safety/risk/insurance/about/automatic/index.html) cover all departments. These programs do not require enrollment forms or premiums. Basic property insurance and some transit programs are examples of automatic programs.

Departments can request additional coverage if necessary. [By-request programs](https://blink.ucsd.edu/safety/risk/insurance/about/byrequest/index.html) require an application, payment of premium, and approval before coverage can begin. Insurance for university-owned automobiles, foreign transit, and fine arts are examples of "by-request" programs.

## Responsibilities

Departments are responsible for uninsured losses and deductibles. Review insurance limitations and exclusions carefully to decide if they fully meet your needs. Risk Management maintains several specialized risk programs and can assist you in negotiating coverage for a particular need.

For more information, see: [Does Your Department Have Adequate Insurance Coverage?](https://blink.ucsd.edu/safety/risk/insurance/about/adequate.html)

## Claims

If a loss occurs, notify [EH&S Risk Management](mailto:ehsrisk@ucsd.edu), (858) 534-2454, immediately, or call the university's third-party administrator directly at 1-800-416-4029 to report a claim.

For property claims, use these step-by-step instructions:

-   [How to File a Fire or Flood Claim](https://blink.ucsd.edu/safety/risk/insurance/about/fire.html)
-   [How to Report Theft, Loss, or Damage to Property](https://blink.ucsd.edu/safety/risk/insurance/about/theft.html)
