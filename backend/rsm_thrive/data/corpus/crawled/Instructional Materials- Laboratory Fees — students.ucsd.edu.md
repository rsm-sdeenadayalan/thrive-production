# Instructional Materials/ Laboratory Fees

Last Updated: November 4, 2025 3:31:34 PM PST

Learn about instructional materials fees or lab fees that students pay for some courses.

## General information about instructional materials/ lab fees:

-   [PPM 120-9](http://adminrecords.ucsd.edu/ppm/docs/120-9.HTML): Course Materials Fees
-   Classes with assessed fees are noted in the [Schedule of Classes](https://act.ucsd.edu/scheduleOfClasses/scheduleOfClassesStudent.htm).
-   The fees are assessed when you enroll in the class.
-   Students who drop a lab course before the end of the second lab meeting are eligible for a refund of the charged lab fee.  Refunds are automatically processed by [Student Financial Solutions](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/).
