# Risk

Location: United States

-   Share This:

Understand how Risk Analysts assess key risks for investment portfolios and devise mitigation strategies

At Goldman Sachs, we believe who you are makes you better at what you do. For us, it’s all about bringing together people who are curious, collaborative, and driven to make things possible for our clients and communities.

Our program will allow you to step into the shoes of a risk analyst and tackle real-world challenges faced by experts at one of the world’s leading investment banks.

By participating in this virtual job simulation, you’ll gain valuable insights into the quantitative side of finance, develop practical skills, and get a glimpse of the exciting career opportunities that await you at Goldman Sachs. Whether you’re a finance enthusiast or just curious about the world of risk, this program is your chance to experience it firsthand.

Interested? Participate in our Risk Virtual Simulation to learn more!

This program is self-paced. It takes approximately **30-60 mins** to complete.

## Skills Learned:

-   **An introduction to risk**  
    Discover the fundamentals of risk assessments by applying them to everyday life decisions
    -   Risk Assessment
    -   Risk Management
    -   Critical Thinking
    -   Financial Literacy
-   **Evaluating client profiles and real estate investments**  
    Now that you understand how risk analysis works in quantitative finance, practice putting these skills to the test
    -   Risk Assessment
    -   Quantitative Analysis
    -   Decision Making
-   **Finish Line**
