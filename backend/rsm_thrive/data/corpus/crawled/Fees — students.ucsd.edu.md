## Special circumstances:

-   [Schedule of Refunds](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/registration/schedule-of-refunds.html): If you withdraw from UC San Diego, you **may** be eligible for a partial or full refund of prepaid fees.
-   [1098-T Forms and Tax Credits for Educational Expenses](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/student-accounts/1098-t.html): You or your parents may be able to claim a credit against your federal income taxes for certain post-secondary educational expenses.
-   [Waiver or Deferment of Fees](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/registration/waiver-or-deferment.html): If you're entitled to a waiver or deferment of fees, the amount will show as a credit on your billing statement.
