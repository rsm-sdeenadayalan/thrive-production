## Canyonview Aquatic Center

### Visitor Parking

-   **Nearest parking facility:** Gilman Parking Structure, located across from the Conrad Prebys Music Center
-   **Directions:** Access the structure by traveling north on Villa La Jolla Drive through the Villa la Jolla Drive and Gilman Drive intersection ([directions via Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uoET1xK3fX3tBT9V8)).
-   **Parking permits:** You can buy a required visitor parking permit at an adjacent parking pay station or by using the zone code "4752" through the [ParkMobile app](https://parkmobile.io/download-app?shortlink=1yyisszu&c=WEB_PARKMOBILE_IO&af_xp=custom&af_dp=https%3A%2F%2Fparkmobile.io%2Fdownload-app&source_caller=ui&pid=QR-code&deep_link_value=parkmobile_io_web_to_app&utm_source=pm_tld). Park only in visitor spaces marked “V” on the ground or EV charging spaces (paid charging session required).
-   **Wayfinding:** Near your parking facility, you will find walking directions to your destination prominently posted.

### Transit

Take the UC San Diego Blue Line Trolley to [Central Campus Station](https://www.sdmts.com/getting-around/departures-and-schedules/departures/100039977/UCSD%20Central%20Campus%20Station,%20Stop%20ID%2077781) one block from your destination. Alternatively, transfer from the MTS and NCTD bus stop at [Gilman Transit Center](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Z6LyDLe5SZbX1XYG7) to Triton Transit’s free [Outside Loop](https://transportation.ucsd.edu/campus/shuttles/loop-o.html) to the [Central Campus Station](https://www.sdmts.com/getting-around/departures-and-schedules/departures/100039977/UCSD%20Central%20Campus%20Station,%20Stop%20ID%2077781).

### Accessibility

Visitors with a disabled persons parking placard or license plate may park free of charge in handicap-accessible spaces in lots P510 or P506 and in EV charging spaces (paid charging session required) throughout campus ([more information](https://transportation.ucsd.edu/commute/permits/accessible.html)).

If your trip to campus requires walking farther than you are able, contact [Triton Mobility](https://transportation.ucsd.edu/campus/shuttles/ada.html) at (858) 534-7422. Rides booked during service hours and more than 24 hours in advance will be prioritized. We will do our best to accommodate unscheduled rides during [regular service hours](https://transportation.ucsd.edu/campus/shuttles/ada.html).

#### Venue Information

Call (858) 534-1430 or email [triton-tix@ucsd.edu](mailto:triton-tix@ucsd.edu).
