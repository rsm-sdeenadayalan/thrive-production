# LinkedIn Skills on the Rise 2026: Sales

Explore LinkedIn’s annual analysis of the fastest-growing skills in sales. Based on workforce and hiring data, this report highlights the technical, analytical, and relationship-building capabilities that sales professionals are increasingly expected to develop.

## Why Use This Resource?

-   Identify the skills most in demand across sales careers
-   Understand how AI is changing modern sales organizations
-   Prioritize skill development for sales and business development roles
-   Benchmark your capabilities against current market trends
-   Prepare for internships and full-time recruiting in customer-facing roles
