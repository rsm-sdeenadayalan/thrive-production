# CaseCoach

![](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/176/2025/07/CaseCoach-Logo-2025.png)

## What is CaseCoach?

CaseCoach is the leading platform for case interview preparation, **trusted by top consulting firms** and 100+ global universities. Designed to double your chances of landing an offer, CaseCoach provides structured, comprehensive support for every step of the case interview process—from mastering frameworks and market sizing to preparing for fit interviews and McKinsey’s PEI.

## Why Use CaseCoach?

CaseCoach brings together all the tools and resources you need to succeed in consulting interviews—whether you’re targeting McKinsey, BCG, Bain, or other top firms. With a clear structure, expert guidance, and access to real interview footage and peer practice opportunities, CaseCoach helps you build confidence, sharpen your problem-solving skills, and avoid common interview pitfalls. It’s the ultimate toolkit for preparing strategically and efficiently so you can perform at your best.

## What You Get with Access

-   **Expert-Led Video Courses:** Case, Fit, Case Math, and McKinsey PEI preparation
-   **100+ Exclusive Case Studies:** With solutions and structured guidance
-   **Mock Interview Practice Room:** Pair with peers from 100+ partner universities
-   **Interactive Drills:** AI-powered tools for math, creativity, market sizing, charts & more
-   **Personalized Dashboard:** Generate a prep plan tailored to your timeline and goals
-   **Real Interview Videos:** See how successful candidates performed and were reviewed

## 🔑 How to Access CaseCoach

CaseCoach is a premium resource reserved for **Rady students who are passionate about pursuing a career in consulting and meet specific qualification criteria**. If you’re serious about consulting and want to take your preparation to the next level, **talk to your career coach** to learn how you can earn access to this exclusive platform.
