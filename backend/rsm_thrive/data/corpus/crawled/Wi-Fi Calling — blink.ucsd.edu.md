# Wi-Fi Calling

Last Updated: June 30, 2023 2:47:13 PM PDT

Read about Wi-Fi Calling on the UC San Diego Wireless Network.

Poor cell phone reception can occur in some UCSD buildings due to the use of dense materials in construction. A new (device dependent) feature now being supported by all major carriers known as Wi-Fi Calling can help alleviate issues caused by the lack of reception.

## About Wi-Fi calling

Maintaining connectivity while roaming between Wi-Fi calling and the cellular provider is dependent upon carrier and mobile device and we have no control over that behavior. For more information see your carriers site.

-   [AT&T](https://www.att.com/shop/wireless/features/wifi-calling.html "AT&T Wi-Fi Calling")
-   [Sprint](http://sprint.co/1MPfyLv "Sprint Wi-Fi Calling")
-   [Verizon](https://www.verizonwireless.com/support/wifi-calling-faqs/#learn "Verizon Wi-Fi Calling")
-   [T-Mobile](https://support.t-mobile.com/docs/DOC-1680 "T-Mobile Wi-Fi Calling")

For more information, contact the [ITS Service Desk](https://servicedesk.ucsd.edu/), (858) 246-4357 or ext. 6-HELP.
