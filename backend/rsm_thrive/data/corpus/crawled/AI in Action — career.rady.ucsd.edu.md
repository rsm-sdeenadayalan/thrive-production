# AI in Action

Location: United States

Master GenAI skills that turn clarity into productivity and workflows into value

Welcome to AI in Action: Prompt Engineering and Workflow Automation at Vista! We are thrilled to have you here.

Vista Equity Partners is a global investment firm recognized for its focus on operational excellence, disciplined execution, and technology-enabled value creation. As a leader in using data and AI to drive business outcomes, Vista equips its teams with cutting-edge tools and practices that accelerate performance across its portfolio of software and technology companies.

During this program, you will get the opportunity to step into the shoes of a Business Operations Associate and complete tasks that replicate the work our Portfolio Operations team does every day. You’ll learn key skills in:

Prompt engineering using Vista’s 4-part methodContext-setting and critical evaluation of AI outputsDesigning repeatable GenAI workflows for professional tasksScaling AI solutions responsibly with guardrails and iteration

We hope this program provides a great resource for you to upskill and strengthen your resume as you explore career options and a potential career at Vista Equity Partners.

This program is self-paced. It takes approximately **1-2 hours** to complete.

-   **Intro & Scenario**  
    Background context and your project team
-   **Build Fluency in Prompt & Contextual Engineering**  
    Learn to write prompts that deliver reliable, professional-grade outputs
    -   Prompt Engineering
    -   Contextual Understanding
    -   Analytical Thinking
    -   Quality Assurance
-   **Apply Prompt Skills to Automate a Professional Workflow**  
    Design repeatable AI workflows that save time, scale impact, and drive operational excellence.
    -   Risk Assessment
    -   Strategic Thinking
    -   Business Acumen
    -   Information Summary
    -   AI Development
-   **Finish Line**
