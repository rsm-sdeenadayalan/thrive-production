# How to Reset Your Business Systems Password

Last Updated: March 31, 2021 5:03:30 PM PDT

Follow these steps if you've forgotten your business systems password or if your password no longer works.

## [1\. Open the Reset My Password screen.](#)

-   From the UCSD System Sign-In page, click [Reset your password?](https://a4.ucsd.edu/resetpw/resetpassword.do)

## [2\. Validate your identity.](#)

Enter all 4 pieces of requested information:

1.  User ID, which can be either of the following:
    -   Your full, official UCSD e-mail address ([aname@ucsd.edu](mailto:aname@ucsd.edu))
        -   If you don't have one, see [Getting a UCSD e-mail address](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/tasks/get-address.html) or contact the [ITS Service Desk](http://servicedesk.ucsd.edu/).
    -   Your mainframe ID (6-digit code)
2.  UCSD employee ID number, which can be found by logging in to [UCPath](https://ucpath.universityofcalifornia.edu/home)
3.  Last 4 digits of your Social Security number
4.  Birth date

Click the **Continue** button.

## [3\. Check your email inbox.](#)

-   The information you provided is affiliated with your e-mail address. A temporary password will be sent to you.
-   Your DSA will receive an e-mail message that you've reset your own password.

**Note:** If you receive an e-mail, but you didn't request a new password, contact your [Department Security Administrator (DSA)](https://iam.ucsd.edu/dsasearch/dsaSearchByEmail) or the [ITS Service Desk](http://servicedesk.ucsd.edu/).

## [4\. Log in again.](#)

-   Use the temporary password the next time you sign on.
-   This password is valid for only one use. When you sign on, you'll be prompted to create a new, permanent password.
-   Choose and confirm a password. Follow the security guidelines on screen. Your password must:
    -   Be 6 to 8 characters of mixed alphabetic, numeric, @, #, or $.
    -   Not contain a common word found in a dictionary

If you need assistance, contact the [ITS Service Desk](https://servicedesk.ucsd.edu/), (858) 246-4357 or ext. 6-HELP.

**Note:** This page has a friendly link that's easy to remember: [http://blink.ucsd.edu/go/reset](http://blink.ucsd.edu/go/reset)
