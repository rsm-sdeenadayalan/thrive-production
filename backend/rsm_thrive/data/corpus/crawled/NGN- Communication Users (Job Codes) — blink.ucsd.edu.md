# NGN: Communication Users (Job Codes)

Last Updated: August 5, 2024 9:26:14 AM PDT

See the payroll job codes for Communication Users used in Next Generation Network (NGN) billing.

**Charges are assessed only for employees whose job codes classify them as Communication Users.** A Communication User is a UCSD employee who makes use of a telephone or campus network during daily work. This status is determined by an individual's payroll job code.

**[Charges are assessed monthly](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/network/NGN/billing/index.html)** per full-time Communication User.

**Notes:**

-   The charge is proportional to the percentage of time employed.

**The job codes [in this file](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/network/NGN/_files/NGN-Job-Codes_8.4.241.xlsx) are designated as Communication Users. This list is subject to revision.**
