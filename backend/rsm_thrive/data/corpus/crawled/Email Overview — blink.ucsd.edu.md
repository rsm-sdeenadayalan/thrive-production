# Email Overview

Last Updated: June 20, 2025 3:17:58 PM PDT

Find a variety of helpful email resources, from common tasks to mobile devices and services.

UC San Diego email requires two-step login (Duo). [Learn more about how two-step login improves email security.](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/two-step-login.html)

## For staff

-   [Common Tasks](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/tasks/index.html): Includes getting set up, configuring your email client, checking email from off-campus, and out-of-office messages.
-   [Email for Mobile Devices](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/tasks/clients/index.html#mobile): Includes accessing email with an iPhone/iPad or Android.
-   [Help With Email](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/tasks/help.html)
-   [Email Encryption](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/encryption/index.html): Review options using Proofpoint Email Encryption, Microsoft 365 Message Encryption, and Google Confidential Mode.
-   [Spam](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/spam/index.html): Learn how you can take action on email messages captured by the UC San Diego Spam Quarantine service.
-   [Email fraud defense using SPF, DKIM, and DMARC](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/email-fraud-defense.html)
-   [Mailing Lists](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/email/services/lists/index.html): Review options for electronic mailing lists using Microsoft Groups, Google Groups, and the Emma email marketing platform.
-   [Network Status](http://status.ucsd.edu/)
-   [Email Appropriate Use](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/help-desk/policies/email/appropriate.html)
-   [Email Policies](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/help-desk/policies/email/index.html)
