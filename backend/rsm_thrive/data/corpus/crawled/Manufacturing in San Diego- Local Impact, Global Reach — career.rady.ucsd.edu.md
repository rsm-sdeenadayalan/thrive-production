# Manufacturing in San Diego: Local Impact, Global Reach

Explore the scale, economic impact, innovation ecosystem, and future growth opportunities of San Diego’s manufacturing sector. Produced by the San Diego Regional Economic Development Corporation (EDC), this report provides data-driven insights into one of the region’s most important industries and highlights the companies, talent, and technologies shaping its future.

### Why Use This Resource?

-   Understand the role manufacturing plays in San Diego’s economy.
-   Identify high-growth industries and employers in the region.
-   Explore career opportunities in manufacturing, technology, life sciences, aerospace, cleantech, and semiconductors.
-   Learn about regional hiring trends, compensation, and workforce needs.
-   Gain industry insights for networking, interviews, employer research, and career exploration.

### What You’ll Find

-   An overview of San Diego’s manufacturing ecosystem, economic impact, and workforce.
-   Employment, wage, and industry growth trends across the region.
-   Analysis of manufacturing opportunities in life sciences, biotechnology, medical devices, technology, aerospace, cleantech, and semiconductors.
-   Profiles and examples of major employers and innovative companies operating in the region.
-   Insights into talent pipelines, industry challenges, and future growth opportunities.
-   Data on the relationship between manufacturing, innovation, and regional economic development.

### Key Findings

-   Manufacturing supports a significant share of San Diego’s private-sector employment and economic output.
-   Average wages in manufacturing exceed the regional average across many occupations and industries.
-   Small businesses play a major role in driving growth throughout the sector.
-   Innovation-focused industries account for a substantial portion of regional manufacturing activity.
-   Cleantech and semiconductor manufacturing are emerging as key areas of future growth and investment.

### How to Use This Resource

Use this report to research industries, employers, and workforce trends before networking conversations, informational interviews, and job applications. Students interested in operations, supply chain, product management, technology, life sciences, finance, consulting, engineering, or strategy can use the data and employer examples to better understand career opportunities and market dynamics in the San Diego region.
