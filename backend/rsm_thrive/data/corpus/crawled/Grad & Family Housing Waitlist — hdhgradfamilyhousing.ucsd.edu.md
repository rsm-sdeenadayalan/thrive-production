# Grad & Family Housing Waitlist

## Immediate Openings Available!

A limited number of apartment spaces are available for immediate and Fall move-in:

-   **Individual Bedrooms in Shared Apartments:** Available at Mesa Nueva, Nuevo West, Nuevo East, One Miramar, and Mesa.
-   **Entire 2-Bedroom Apartments:** Available at South Mesa, One Miramar, and Nuevo East.

Students interested in on-campus housing are encouraged to contact [gradfamilyhousing@ucsd.edu](mailto:gradfamilyhousing@ucsd.edu) or apply today.

## Create a new Application or Update your Current Application

Please carefully read the options below before clicking a button so that you are certain on which status applies to you:

### With Campus Account:

Students, Faculty and Staff with a campus account (AD campus credentials):

Create a new application or update an existing one through the Waitlist Portal tab below.  Login with your AD campus credentials.  Once you are logged in, select the Grad and Family Housing Waitlist category and select the appropriate link.

[Log In Using AD campus credentials](https://hdh-web.ucsd.edu/ssoAD/RMSportal/Home/Index/A "Resident Portal Login")

### Faculty and Staff without a campus account (without AD credentials):

1.  You will first need to request access by clicking on the "Request a Temporary Username and Password" button.  Requests will be reviewed for eligibility and you will be notified via email in about 48 hours.
    
    [Request a Temporary Username and Password](https://pavo.ucsd.edu/Page/RequesttoCreateGFHWaitlistTempAccount)
    
2.  Once approved, please click the "Log In With Temporary Username and Password" button.
    
    [Log In With Temporary Username and Password](https://pavo.ucsd.edu/Page/GFHWaitlistLandingPage)
