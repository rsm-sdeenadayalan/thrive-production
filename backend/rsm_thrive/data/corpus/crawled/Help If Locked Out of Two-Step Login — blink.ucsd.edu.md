# Help If Locked Out of Two-Step Login

Last Updated: October 14, 2022 3:55:55 PM PDT

Learn what to do if your device isn't available or you find yourself locked out.

Common Issues and Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| I can't access systems or the VPN with two-step login because I forgot my device or it's otherwise unavailable.  | Contact Service Desk by phone or visit the walk-up desk at AP&M to receive a bypass code. To receive a bypass code you must call or visit in person and will be required to verify your identity.  |
| I'm locked out because of too many failed two-step login attempts.  | 
Your account auto-unlocks after 10 minutes. For immediate access, use any method to contact the Service Desk.

A lockout occurs after 10 consecutive failed login attempts. Common lockout causes are attempts that are ignored, actively denied, or incorrectly entered; or if Duo is attempting to send a push notification to a phone with no app installed.

 |

-   Submit a ticket online at [servicedesk.ucsd.edu](https://servicedesk.ucsd.edu/)
-   Email [servicedesk@ucsd.edu](mailto:servicedesk@ucsd.edu)
-   Phone: Call (858) 246-4357

        -   Monday through Friday: 7 a.m. – 10 p.m.
        -   Saturday and Sunday, noon - 3 p.m.
    -   Classes **not** in session: 
        -   Monday through Friday: 7 a.m. - 7 p.m. 
        -   No weekend hours
-   Visit walk up desk at AP&M building, room 1313 (8 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. Monday - Friday)

## After Hours Help
