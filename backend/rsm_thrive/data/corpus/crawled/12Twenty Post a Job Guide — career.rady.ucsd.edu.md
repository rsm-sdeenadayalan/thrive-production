# 12Twenty Post a Job Guide

## Getting Started

1.  Login to your [**Rady 12Twenty account**](https://rady-ucsd.12twenty.com/hire).
2.  From the home page, click _**OCI and Job Listings**_ from the left navigation menu.
3.  Click the **_\+ Post a Job_ button** in the top right corner of your screen.

* * *

## Creating Your Job Posting

A new “Create Job Posting” window will appear. Enter in the details of the job post, some important fields to note are:

-   **Work Authorization Requirement**
    -   If your job is only open to students who have permanent US work authorization, then select _Permanent US Work Authorization Required_ only
        -   This choice means you are not considering applicants that need any type of immigration sponsorship (additional work authorization or permanent work authorization) now or in the future to work in the United States. This includes, but IS NOT LIMITED TO: F1-CPT, F1-OPT, H-1B, TN, L-1, J-1, etc.
    -   If your job is open to domestic _and_ international students, meaning all students are eligible, including international students eligible for CPT and OPT while on an F-1 Visa (H-1B sponsorship may be considered, but is not guaranteed), then select _All Work Authorization Accepted_

-   **Application Document Requirements**
    -   Here is where you will select which documents you request students to submit to apply to the position. Click _+Add Additional Document Type_ if you’d like to request another document from students. In order for that document to be required to apply, make sure to click the _Is Required?_ checkbox next to the document name.
    -   Please note that Resume is a required document for all job applicants in 12Twenty.

-   **Primary Job Contact**
    -   This field allows you to input the name and information of the main source of contact on the job posting. **If you do NOT want students to have certain information within this section, make sure the _Make Visible to Students_ checkbox is unchecked.**

-   **Eligibility**
    -   Here is where you will choose which students are able to see and apply to your job posting. You are able to refine your audience based on two factors: student group and work authorization.
        -   **By Student Group:**
            -   Students are grouped by **program** and **graduation date**. You are able to select all groups that apply, but if you would like all current Rady graduate students to be able to apply, you only need to choose the _All Current Students_ student group.
        -   **By Work Authorization:**
            -   In conjunction with the student groups, you can refine who may view and apply to your job posting by their work authorization status. If your job is only open to permanent US residents, then only select _Permanent Work Authorization_. If you are also open to international students, then you can also select _Non-Permanent Work Authorization_.
            -   If you do not complete this field, then by default _all_ students who fall under your selected student group(s) will be able to view and apply to the posting.

Click _Submit_ once complete. Your job posting will be flagged for the Career Center staff to review and approve to post to students. You will receive an email once the job posting has been approved; if there were any issues with the posting, a career center member will reach out to you with details.
