# How to Enroll in Summer Session: Visitors

Last Updated: May 11, 2026 3:57:01 PM PDT

Find out how to register for Summer Session classes if you're not a current UC San Diego student.

## How to apply:

### 1. Make sure you're eligible.

You can enroll in Summer Session if you fit in one of these categories and do not have outstanding fees on any UC San Diego account:

-   Are a UCSD employee
-   Are an admitted fall student at UCSD or another UC campus
-   Have completed high school or are at least 18 years old
-   Are currently enrolled in a college, including another UC campus, and can return to your home campus for the next fall academic term
-   Current high school student with a minimum 3.3 GPA (see [Summer Session website](https://summersession.ucsd.edu/enroll/eligibility.html) for eligibility requirements)

### 2. Understand Summer Session policies and procedures.

-   Visit the [Summer Session website](https://summersession.ucsd.edu/) to read important information regarding enrollment, enrollment schedule, fees, deadlines, refunds, and more. You're responsible for knowing this information.
-   If you're currently enrolled in a college, including another UC campus, contact your home campus for information on transferring units, or visit the [ASSIST website.](https://www.assist.org/)

### 3. Find classes and get approvals.

-   Go to the [Schedule of Classes](https://act.ucsd.edu/cgi-bin/tritonlink.pl/2/students/academic/classes/schedule_of_classes.pl) to see a list of Summer Session courses and their fees. Make sure you check for prerequisites and restriction codes.
-   If you plan to enroll in more than 15 credits, you must get approval from the [Summer Session director](https://summersession.ucsd.edu/about-us/index.html).

### 4. Apply for Summer Session.

-   If you've never attended UC San Diego, you'll need to create your [user ID and password](https://itsweb.ucsd.edu/manage-sso/summer) to enroll via the [Summer Session online application](https://itsweb.ucsd.edu/manage-sso/summer).
-   If you know your User ID and password, you can complete the [Summer Session online application](https://act.ucsd.edu/studentSummerApplication2/application).
-   If you don't know your user ID/ PID and password/ PAC, call 858-534-3150.
-   If you have your user ID/ PID, but don't remember your password, you can use the [Password Change tool](https://password.ucsd.edu/).

**Note:** If you miss an enrollment deadline, you must pay a late fee.

### 5. Enroll in classes.

Use [WebReg](https://act.ucsd.edu/webreg2) to enroll in classes after you successfully submit your application.

**Note:** If you miss an enrollment deadline, you must pay a late fee.

### 6. Pay your fees.

Your registration in Summer Session is complete when you pay your fees. For information, see [Summer Session Fees and Payment](https://summersession.ucsd.edu/tuition/index.html).

Questions?

-   If you're a visiting student, contact the [Registrar's Office](https://registrar.ucsd.edu/), (858) 534-3150.
-   If you're a non-UC international student, contact [International Programs](https://extension.ucsd.edu/international-programs/home), (858) 534-6784.
