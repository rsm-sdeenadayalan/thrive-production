# Rady Resume Guide

The Rady resume format and this guide are the result of input from business recruiters, Rady corporate partners, and the Rady Career Management Center (CMC). The goal of this guide is to help you create a successful default resume for ongoing use in your job search. Once a default Rady resume is created, evaluated through VMock, and saved into 12Twenty, it can be adjusted to produce additional tailored resumes for future applications.

* * *

## Rady Resume Formatting

The format will allow you to highlight skills that are relevant to your career goals and allow employers to find the experience they seek. Consistency is key.

**Fonts** | Use a standard font (Helvetica, Calibri, or Times New Roman). Use the same font throughout the resume (and cover letter). Font size: 10-12, but 11 is best.

**Margins & Page Length** | Use 0.5” to 1” margins to keep your resume to **one page**.

**Items to Avoid** | Use of colors beyond black and white. Personal information such as age, citizenship status, marital status or photos.

**Contact Information** | Size 10-12, but 11 is best. Use your Rady email and be sure to check your email and voicemail often for responses from employers. Include your first and last name, telephone number, e-mail address, and customized LinkedIn URL (Google “customize linkedin url” to learn how). A preferred name may be included. Example: Xiu Ying (Julie) Zhang. For your address, it is now standard to just include city, state.

**Sections/Headers** | Have the same font, size, emphasis (bold, capitalization, etc.) for each. See details below and resume templates.

**Objective** | **Do not include** on default Rady Resume. Use only on tailored resumes but it is optional. _If choosing to use one_, state a brief, focused objective with:  
A) title and/or type of position  
B) field, industry, company name (optional)  
C) your skills or expertise relevant to the specific position  
Omit if including application or cover letter with resume.

**Summary of Qualifications** | **Not recommended for Rady Resume**. For other resumes: optional. Summarize your skills or expertise relevant to the industry or type of positions you generally apply to with that resume. Omit if including application or cover letter with resume.

* * *

### Education Section

**Place at the top of your Rady Resume**. Education at the top explains why you are not working, is often more relevant to the position, and allows you to showcase key skills and qualifications recently gained.

**School Name:  
**Rady School of Management  
University of California, San Diego, CA

**Degree Name:** Write out your full degree name. Master of Business Administration, Master of Finance, etc.

**Top Honors**: List after your degree and before your concentration in italics. Do not capitalize. Examples: summa cum laude, magna cum laude, cum laude, or with honors.

**Anticipated Graduation Date**: Month and Year: June 2024 or 6/2024

**GPA and/or GMAT**: Consider including only if 3.5 or higher (GPA); 700+ (GMAT)

**Academic Awards/Scholarships**: List the award name(s) bullet points directly under the school where you received the recognition. If the purpose behind the award is not obvious by its title, you may provide a brief description. Examples: Ford Scholarship Recipient, 2015 (Awarded for top 10 students with highest GPA).

**Coursework**: Optional: list 4 to 6 classes (by course title) that are relevant to the type of jobs you are seeking. Helps showcase industry keywords. Exclude academic department, course number, grades or the term you took the class.

### Experience

This can include paid, unpaid, part-time, and/or full-time employment and internships.

**The Goal:  
**Pick experiences that showcase your relevant or transferable business skills, accomplishments and knowledge. For tailored resumes: seek out keywords for job descriptions to help you understand the needs of the industry and employer you are targeting.

**Experience Formatting:  
**List positions in reverse chronological order. Only use bullet points and not complete sentences or paragraphs. Use the correct verb tense: present tense for current position; past tense for former positions. If the company is potentially unknown, include a single sentence about the company – its industry, size, and/or if it is domestic or global.

**Accomplishment Statements (bullet points):  
**Begin with an action verb followed by details on how you completed the task (using keywords), and end with the accomplishment or result. This communicates to the reader that you have achieved results while developing transferable skills and career competencies. Avoid vague qualitative terms such as “large” or “many”. Instead use numbers where appropriate to clearly describe your accomplishments (Examples: “led a team of nine engineers”, “increased sales by 40%”, or “saved the company $100K+).

* * *

**Projects** | Optional section. Consider listing a few relevant academic/course projects or research, Lab to Market, or your capstone. If relevant and significant, you may also consider any projects as part of your unpaid leadership work with student organizations and/or volunteer/community service. Include bullet points to showcase all relevant skills and accomplishments.

* * *

**Specialized Skills** | List 3 to 6 skills, qualifications, and abilities that relate to your industry of interest. Examples: needed language skills, laboratory/technical abilities, computer programs, and licenses or certifications. Publications & Patents can be listed here or in an additional optional section. Only send samples if they are requested. See templates below.

* * *

**Professional Affiliations** | Optional section. Keep the list current and brief, with primarily active memberships and interests that highlight relevant activities. These items will show you to be well-rounded and someone who gets involved with his/her community as well as your leadership qualities.
