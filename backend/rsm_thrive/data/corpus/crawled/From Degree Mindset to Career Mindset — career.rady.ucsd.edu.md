# From Degree Mindset to Career Mindset

Success in the job search depends on more than academic achievement. This worksheet helps students shift from a student mindset to a professional mindset by examining common assumptions about recruiting, interviewing, and employer expectations. Through guided reflection and career readiness exercises, students can identify habits and perspectives that support long-term career success.

### Why Use This Resource?

-   Develop a more effective approach to recruiting and interviewing.
-   Understand what employers value beyond academic performance.
-   Identify limiting beliefs that may impact your job search.
-   Build confidence through preparation and self-awareness.
-   Strengthen your professional mindset before interviews and networking conversations.

### What You’ll Find

-   A comparison of common “Degree Mindset” and “Career Mindset” perspectives.
-   Self-reflection exercises to evaluate your current approach to career development.
-   Interview and hiring statistics that provide insight into employer expectations.
-   Questions designed to challenge assumptions about the job search process.
-   Guidance on developing the preparation, resilience, and professionalism employers seek.

### How to Use This Resource

Complete the reflection exercises and mindset assessments to evaluate how you currently approach your career search. Review the hiring insights and mindset comparisons to identify areas for growth, then create an action plan to improve your interview preparation, networking strategy, and overall career readiness.
