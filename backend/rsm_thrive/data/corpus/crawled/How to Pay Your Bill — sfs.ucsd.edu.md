## HOW E-BILL WORKS

UC San Diego sends an E-Bill notice to your University-issued e-mail address when your bill is ready to be viewed and paid. As a part of sustainability initiatives, UC San Diego does not issue paper bills.

Your student account displays charges as well as credits (such as financial aid and payments) to your account.

**E-BILL PRO TIPS**

-   Check your official UC San Diego e-mail account regularly so you don't miss your E-Bill notifications.
-   E-Bills are sent **monthly** or anytime there is financial activity, a balance is due, or a credit is available on your student account.
-   Authorize a parent or other payer to receive E-Bill notifications and make payments on your behalf.
-   Check out our guide on **[How to Navigate Your Account](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/_files/NavigatingYourAccount.pdf)**.
-   A copy of the information contained on the back page of the billing statement can be found [here](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/_files/statementback_paper2023_04_26.pdf).
