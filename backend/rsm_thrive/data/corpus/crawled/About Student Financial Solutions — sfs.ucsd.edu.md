# About Student Financial Solutions

## Mission

* * *

To support the financial needs  of students, parents, and organizations through an integrated services approach.

## Vision

* * *

To enrich our students' lives through programs and solutions that educate, empower, and encourage life-long financial health.

**Our [core values](https://sfs.ucsd.edu/_images/core-values.png) guide us in everything we do.**

## People First

Everything we do comes from our intrinsic motivation to guarantee the best possible outcome to the people we serve by being empathetic, compassionate, and open-minded.

## Purposeful Interactions

We believe that going above and beyond the needs and expectations of our customers are paramount to creating positive experiences at every touchpoint.

## Building Pathways

We construct meaningful work through collaboration, connection, and harmony to deliver unified solutions with our campus partners.

## Progressive Change

Our never-stop-learning mindset allows us to address present challenges and identify potential breakthroughs.
