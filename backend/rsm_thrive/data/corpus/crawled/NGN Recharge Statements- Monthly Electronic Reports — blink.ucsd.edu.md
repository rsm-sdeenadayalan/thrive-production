# NGN Recharge Statements: Monthly Electronic Reports

## Telecommunications / NGN Recharge Statements: Monthly Electronic Reports

Last Updated: August 4, 2024 1:49:17 PM PDT

Learn how departments can view their monthly Telecommunications/ NGN recharge statements electronically.

As a response to many requests from campus departments, monthly Telecommunications/ NGN recharge statements became available in electronic format beginning July 2006 and are no longer available in hard copy format.

To access your monthly Telecom statement electronically:

-   **Go to** [Telecom Recharge Details Report](https://bianalytics.ucsd.edu/ibmcognos/bi/?pathRef=.public_folders%2FTelecom%2FReports%2FTelecom%2BRecharge%2BDetails%2BReport) (login required)

To access your monthly NGN statement electronically:

-   **Go to** [NGN Recharge Summary Report](https://bianalytics.ucsd.edu/ibmcognos/bi/?perspective=authoring&pathRef=.public_folders%2FNGN%2FCampus%2BReports%2FNGN%2BDetails%2BPortal&id=iD8F96E20EADF4CBCB16874CD6BC0D5A4&objRef=iD8F96E20EADF4CBCB16874CD6BC0D5A4&action=run&format=HTML&cmPropStr=%7B%22id%22%3A%22iD8F96E20EADF4CBCB16874CD6BC0D5A4%22%2C%22type%22%3A%22report%22%2C%22defaultName%22%3A%22NGN%20Details%20Portal%22%2C%22permissions%22%3A%5B%22execute%22%2C%22read%22%2C%22traverse%22%5D%7D) (login required)
-   **See** [NGN: Sample Recharge Statement - Pre-July 2024](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/network/NGN/reports/sample.html)
-   **See** [NGN: Sample Recharge Statement - Post-July 2024](https://blink.ucsd.edu/technology/network/NGN/reports/sample.html)

If you have questions, please submit a [Services & Support](https://support.ucsd.edu/its) ticket - Select service: NGN or Telecom Management System.
